ABOUT

They know. Well, that is the idea.

Fictional grandmothers, well into their seventies, a very real Riviera and very little patience for your cagades.

The idea

Les Niçoises do not exist. At least, not the women in the photograph. In our heads, they are well into their seventies and have watched a few million visitors, Chanel bags taken out for gelato, iPhones held at arm’s length and people discovering Èze at 1 pm in August before complaining about the crowds. Hu. They have seen worse.

The site is real. It selects beaches, restaurants, hotels, cultural places and day trips across the French Riviera. Information is researched and checked. If somewhere has not been personally visited, we do not pretend otherwise.

We prefer a clear opinion to twenty adjectives. A good place can be expensive. A bad one can too. The view may be magnificent and lunch frankly mediocre. And a Chanel bag, an iPhone and forty-eight hours on the Riviera still do not constitute local knowledge. You may be offended, pichoun. Dégun will hold it against you.

HOUSE RULE

Everybody else rolls out the red carpet. We do not. We will help you, save you time and spare you a few cagades. But if your idea is terrible, we will say so. At our age, flattery is exhausting.

A tiny lexicon. We are not translating it every time.
Mèfi: watch out. Pichoun: little one, affectionately. Cagade: a stupid avoidable mistake. Dégun: nobody. Ficanas: gossips and busybodies. Empégué: the person who looks completely out of it, particularly behind the wheel. Niocou: fool. Paillassou: not exactly the sharpest knife in the drawer. Hu: put it wherever you like.

If a page is still short, that is deliberate. We prefer three useful choices to a list of thirty names written for Google and forgotten by everyone else.

Independent editorial selection. Practical details change, sometimes between coffee and the bill.
