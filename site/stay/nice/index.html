Choose the trip before the room

Where should you stay in Nice?

Thirteen hotels, four trip styles, from sensible mid-range choices to real luxury. Decide what the hotel needs to do first, then decide how much you want to spend.

13 selected hotelsBest base · no-car trips15 September 2026

Step 1 · Choose the area

Four areas of Nice. Four very different holidays.

Central Nice is walkable, but where you sleep still shapes your days: trains, beach, evenings, noise. Pick the area first, then the hotel.

  1. 01

    Carré d’Or & Promenade

    First-timers who want the sea and everything close.

    +Beach, restaurants and Old Nice on foot.

    −Pricier, especially facing the sea.

    Our picks here Villa Victoria · Le Grimaldi · Boscolo · Maison Albar · Anantara Plaza · Palais de la Méditerranée · Negresco

  2. 02

    Jean-Médecin & the station

    Car-free trips with lots of day trips by train.

    +Station, tram and shops at the door.

    −Less charm. Streets by the station are dull after dark.

    Our picks here Hotel 66 · Boutique Hôtel Nice Côte d’Azur · Apollinaire

  3. 03

    Old Nice

    Atmosphere, dinners out, everything on foot.

    +Markets, lanes, the sea a few minutes away.

    −Lively at night. Tricky with suitcases.

    Our picks here Hôtel du Couvent · Beau Rivage · La Pérouse

  4. 04

    The Port

    A real neighbourhood feel and swims along the eastern coast.

    +Place Garibaldi, restaurants, tram to the airport.

    −Further from the station for daily train trips.

    No pick of ours here yet. Closest: La Pérouse, at Rauba Capeu.

The Mametas verdict

First time in Nice? Sleep between Nice-Ville station and Place Masséna.

Old Nice if you came for the evenings. The Port for a more local, laid-back stay.
