HOW THE BASES DIFFER

Same coast. Different trip.

The prettiest place is not automatically the best base. Annoying, we know. Pick the place that makes your actual holiday easier. Beauty can sulk for five minutes.

Entre nous · the Mametas

Antibes and Juan-les-Pins share a town hall, not a bedtime: ramparts and old lanes on one side, sand and late bars on the other. Book where you’ll want to walk home after dinner.

Nice

Best first base. Not sacred. Just annoyingly useful, with trains, restaurants, culture and a real city after dinner.

Villefranche & Cap-Ferrat

Best for beauty. Beauty wins. Logistics grumble a little. That can be a very good trade.

Antibes

Best balance. Old town, sand and useful trains. Very little need to perform.

Cannes

Best for polish. Another confession. Compact, sandy and very serious about the hotel being part of the holiday.

Monaco

Best for spectacle. Easy day trip, expensive base. We would not make it the default unless Monaco itself is the point.

Menton

Best for a slower east. Softer, quieter and the perfect counterpoint to Monaco.

Worth leaving the main line for

Four detours that change the trip.

Not every good Riviera day starts with a seaside stop. These four earn the extra logistics when the mood is right.

Èze

Best for the panorama. Small, steep and spectacular. Timing matters almost as much as the view.

Saint-Paul-de-Vence

Best for art inland. Ramparts, galleries and Fondation Maeght make it more than a pretty-village stop.

Saint-Tropez

Only if you really want it. A full-day expedition from Nice.

Grasse

Best for perfume. Make your own fragrance in the town that taught the trade. A day trip, not a base.
