SPRING

Walk first. Swim later.

Air: roughly 17°C by day in April, rising into the mid-20s by June. Sea: around 15°C in April and about 22°C in June. Excellent for walking and culture; the beach can look more convincing than it feels until late May or June.

SUMMER

Beach weather, earlier starts.

Expect high-20s daytime heat as the norm, with hotter spells possible; the sea is usually in the low-to-mid 20s. Start long walks early and treat what genuinely sells out as bookings. See what to book →

AUTUMN

September is still summer-adjacent.

September is still a sea month: water is commonly around 23°C before cooling through October. Later in autumn, keep an indoor plan ready. Use the rain plan →

WINTER

Choose a city.

Nice, Menton and Monaco remain credible urban bases. The sea falls towards the mid-teens and some seasonal addresses pause. Think culture, food, coastal light and a real indoor backup. Keep the rain plan handy →
