SPRING

Walk first. Swim later.

Air: roughly 17°C by day in April, rising into the mid-20s by June. Sea: around 15°C in April and about 22°C in June. Excellent for walking and culture; the beach can look more convincing than it feels until late May or June.

SUMMER

Beach weather, earlier starts.

Expect high-20s daytime heat as the norm, with hotter spells possible; the sea is usually in the low-to-mid 20s. Start long walks early and book ahead whatever really sells out. See what to book →

AUTUMN

September is still summer-adjacent.

September is still a sea month: water is commonly around 23°C before cooling through October. Later in autumn, keep an indoor plan ready. Use the rain plan →

WINTER

Choose a city.

Nice, Menton and Monaco remain credible urban bases. The sea falls towards the mid-teens and some seasonal places close for the winter. Think culture, food, coastal light and a real indoor backup. Keep the rain plan handy →

Nice in winter: mild, not warm

In January Nice averages 13°C by day and 6°C at night, with about 157 hours of sunshine (Météo-France normals, 1991-2020). The sea is around 13-14°C: a swim is for the brave.

The ski resorts are two to two and a half hours away by bus. In the 2025-26 season, Lignes d’Azur ran “100% Neige” ski buses from Nice to Isola 2000, Auron and La Colmiane (€10 one way, €14 return, booking required by noon the day before); check the new season before you count on it. And February is the busy month: the Nice Carnival → runs from 9 to 28 February 2027 and the Menton Lemon Festival → from 13 to 28 February 2027. Book the hotel before the parade tickets.
