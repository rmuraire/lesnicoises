Mametas in one sentence

Mametas is an independent bilingual French Riviera guide created and edited by Renaud Muraire, a Niçois, for first-time visitors who want clear decisions rather than endless lists.

02

Two tools, one way of deciding

Mametas Riviera Fit chooses a base. Mametas Hotel Fit narrows the hotel selection once geography is settled. Reality Check is not a third tool: it is the part of each recommendation that spells out car, budget, season and friction.
