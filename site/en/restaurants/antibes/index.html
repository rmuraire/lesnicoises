RESTAURANTS · ANTIBES

Where to eat in Antibes

Old Antibes, Juan-les-Pins, Cap d’Antibes: ten addresses doing genuinely different jobs. That is the point.

MAMETAS SAYS

Do not choose the same restaurant after La Gravette, a day in Old Antibes and a big night on the Cap. Same town, different brief.

PRICE

€ easy on the wallet · €€ mid-range · €€€ expensive / occasion · €€€€ big night. Relative positioning.

Decide in 20 seconds

Old town / easy: Le Vauban (€€), La Cafetière Fêlée (€€), L’Arazur (€€€). Fish / sea: Maison de Bacon (€€€€), Les Pêcheurs (€€€€). Stylish Mediterranean: Miraé (€€€). Big night: Le Figuier de Saint-Esprit (€€€€), Louroc (€€€€), Amarines (€€€€), La Passagère (€€€€).

Old Antibes

Le Vauban

€€ · traditional · polished bistro

Best for: a central, classic dinner without palace theatre.

Seasonal French cooking in a well-established Old Antibes house. But: no Cap d’Antibes drama, but a much easier evening if your day stays in town.

18 rue du Marc

La Cafetière Fêlée

€€ · French-Asian fusion · small table

Best for: leaving the Mediterranean register for a genuinely independent small restaurant.

Julien Fiengo cooks home-made French-Asian fusion largely by himself, in a 30-seat restaurant open since 2008. But: limited covers and an artisanal rhythm. Patience is part of the contract.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗
8 rue des Palmiers

L’Arazur

€€€ · gastronomic · intimate

Best for: a more worked dinner in Old Antibes without going full palace.

Fresh produce, seasonal menus, a small terrace and a stone-vaulted room. But: occasion budget and advance booking are sensible.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗
Cap d’Antibes

Maison de Bacon

€€€€ · fish / seafood · view

Best for: a seafood lunch where the setting matters as much as the cooking.

A sea-facing institution since 1948, now built around marine and Mediterranean cooking. But: you are paying for the panorama too. At least the panorama has done the work.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗
Ramparts

Le Figuier de Saint-Esprit

€€€€ · gastronomic · big dinner in town

Best for: making dinner the main event without leaving Antibes.

Christian Morisset’s gastronomic cooking remains closely tied to the region. But: time, money and booking. Do not make the return train your dessert course.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗
Cap d’Antibes

Les Pêcheurs

€€€€ · Mediterranean / seafood · dinner

Best for: a fish-led dinner rooted in the local coast.

Daily-selected seafood and Côte d’Azur produce with broader influences. But: a destination in itself, less logical if your entire evening is centred on Old Antibes.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗
Hôtel du Cap-Eden-Roc

Louroc

€€€€ · Provençal / Mediterranean · palace

Best for: the deliberate grand-hotel experience facing the Mediterranean.

Sébastien Broda works with seasons, regional producers and elegant Provençal cooking. But: dress code, budget and ritual. Do not come looking for a secret neighbourhood bistro.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗
Villa Miraé

Miraé by Mauro Colagreco

€€€ · Mediterranean · terrace

Best for: a polished occasion with a sunnier, less ceremonial tone.

Seasonal produce, French and Italian Riviera influences and a terrace open to the Cap. But: still an occasion restaurant, however relaxed the mood.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗
Villa Miraé

Amarines by Mauro Colagreco

€€€€ · creative · gastronomic dinner

Best for: a dinner built around seasons, local produce and the terrace.

Mediterranean fine dining with local produce, sustainable catch and a deliberately more spontaneous spirit. But: the budget still belongs to a full experience.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗
Hôtel Belles Rives · Juan-les-Pins

La Passagère

€€€€ · creative / seafood · Art Deco

Best for: the big night in Juan-les-Pins with the sea in both the setting and the plate.

Fish, shellfish and seafood in an Art Deco dining room and terrace facing the Lérins Islands. But: you are choosing a Riviera evening as much as a meal.
