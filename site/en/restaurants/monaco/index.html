RESTAURANTS · MONACO

Where to eat in Monaco

Eight options, because in Monaco “chic” is not enough information. Pick the register first, then the bill.

MAMETAS SAYS

Monaco is very good at selling ceremony. Our job is to tell you when it deserves your evening and when oysters on a wooden bench win the argument.

Les Perles de Monte-Carlo

€€ · Oysters and seafood · Very casual

Best for: lunch in Fontvieille.

Monaco without the starched tablecloth. But: not a dressed-up dinner; the house asks you to book.

La Table d’Élise

€€€ · French land and sea · Elegant, warm

Best for: a serious dinner without palace theatre.

Jean-Luc L’Hourre brings together Breton roots, seafood and flavours of the South. But: less Monaco spectacle, more actual restaurant. It happens.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗

Beefbar

€€€ · Meat and grills · Design-led, lively

Best for: carnivores and a contemporary dinner.

Born in Monaco in 2005, the concept puts exceptional beef at the centre in a more relaxed setting. But: the concept is part of the experience.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗

Rampoldi Monte-Carlo

€€€€ · Italian / Mediterranean · Elegant

Best for: Carré d’Or.

A Monaco institution since 1946. But: setting and service are part of the price.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗

Yoshi

€€€€ · Japanese · Intimate

Best for: high-end Japanese dinner.

About forty seats, nigiri, teppanyaki, black cod and sake inside Hôtel Métropole. But: full luxury register, even if the room turns the volume down.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗

Les Ambassadeurs by Christophe Cussac

€€€€ · Mediterranean · Palace

Best for: classical fine dining.

Christophe Cussac builds his plates around three flavours and the Mediterranean basin. But: elegant dress, dinner only and a genuine occasion.

Official source ↗Google Maps ↗

Pavyllon Monte-Carlo

€€€€ · Modern · Palace, more open in feel

Best for: fine dining with a terrace.

Yannick Alléno’s table at Hôtel Hermitage also offers a lunch format that is easier to read than full evening ceremony. But: “more accessible” remains a very Monaco concept.

Le Louis XV - Alain Ducasse

€€€€ · Mediterranean · Ultimate big night

Best for: making dinner the whole programme.

The Hôtel de Paris table remains the most ceremonial proposition here, deeply inspired by the Riviera. But: time, money and planning. Nothing here is pretending otherwise.
