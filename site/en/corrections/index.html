CORRECTIONS & UPDATES

A good recommendation can still contain an old fact.

Hours, fares, access, closures and transport change. Mametas dates its checks and corrects documented facts when they move.

01

Report the fact.

Send the page, the detail that looks wrong and, where possible, an official or first-party source.

02

We check before changing it.

A documented correction is tested against available sources. A personal preference remains a personal preference.

03

Dates matter.

When an important practical fact is reviewed, the page check date should reflect that new pass.

04

The verdict may stay the same.

Correcting a fare or an opening time does not automatically rewrite the editorial judgement. Facts and recommendations remain separate layers.
