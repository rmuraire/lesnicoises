HOTEL · Menton

Hôtel Napoléon Menton

The more holiday-shaped Menton option: sea, a little more space and less need to pretend the hotel is merely functional.

Who it suits

Napoléon is for travellers who want Menton to be the stay. The eastern position and seaside feel make it a stronger choice for slowing down than for maximising daily mileage.

Mèfi: the trade-off

That is not a weakness if your plan is beach, old town, gardens and a Monaco outing. It is only a weakness if you intend to commute to Cannes every morning for sport.
