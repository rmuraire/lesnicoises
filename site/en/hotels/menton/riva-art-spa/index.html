Who it suits

Riva works well for a no-car Menton test because the seafront is outside the door, the station is roughly 900 m away and the hotel lists accessible facilities. The rooftop spa is a real bonus, but it is not why the transport side works.

The catch

Nine hundred metres with luggage is still nine hundred metres. If that walk is the deciding factor, use a taxi from the station or choose a more station-centric address.
