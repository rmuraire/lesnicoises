HOTEL · Menton

Villa Genesis

Menton has lemons. It also has a five-star villa. Try to contain your surprise.

Around 100 metres from the sea according to the hotel, Villa Genesis occupies a renovated period villa in Menton with pool, garden and bar.

The address feels far more residential than Monaco's grand hotels and lets you use Menton, nearby Italy and the eastern Riviera without necessarily paying the symbolic surcharge attached to Casino Square.

THE MAMETAS VERDICT

An elegant, calmer base for Menton. The kind of town where walking to the market is still considered a normal activity. Enjoy it.

Why we keep it

Menton works extremely well as a rail base for Monaco, Roquebrune and Ventimiglia while remaining a destination in its own right.

The pool gives the villa a genuine holiday feel without turning it into an oversized resort.

Who it suits

  • You want to explore Menton and the eastern Riviera.
  • You prefer a villa to an urban palace.
  • Pool, garden and calm matter more than nightlife.

Mèfi: watch out if...

  • You are coming solely to go out in Monaco every night.
  • You want Menton's old town immediately outside your bed.
  • You require a private hotel beach.
