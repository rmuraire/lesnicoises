HOTEL · Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat

Royal-Riviera

The Riviera for people who prefer Cap-Ferrat to Monaco's noise. Understandable.

At the entrance to Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat, Royal-Riviera is a five-star hotel with 94 rooms and suites, an outdoor pool, seasonal private beach and Mediterranean gardens.

The location is particularly useful if you want to sit between Nice and Monaco without sleeping in the middle of either. Beaulieu-sur-Mer and Villa Kérylos are right next door.

THE MAMETAS VERDICT

The calm, very-Riviera choice. You do not need 120-decibel music to prove you are on holiday, pichoun.

Why we keep it

The beach and pool make the hotel self-contained enough to actually slow down, while Beaulieu station also makes Nice or Monaco easy to reach.

It is a strong compromise if you want an elegant seaside stay but find Saint-Tropez a little too much like full-time social performance.

Who it suits

  • You want to alternate beach, pool and small excursions.
  • You prefer calm to city-centre energy.
  • You want Nice and Monaco within easy reach, but not outside your window.

Mèfi: watch out if...

  • You want dozens of restaurants outside the door every night.
  • You are coming purely for nightlife.
  • Your definition of a holiday requires a much more urban address.
