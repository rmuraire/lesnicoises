HOTEL · Roquebrune-Cap-Martin

The Maybourne Riviera

A piece of architecture that seems to have forgotten that a cliff is not normally a place for furniture.

Clinging to the cliffs of Roquebrune-Cap-Martin above Monaco, The Maybourne Riviera is an uncompromising contemporary luxury hotel with very open Mediterranean views.

Stays include access to Surrenne Riviera wellness facilities and, in season according to the hotel, a private beach plus shuttle service to Monaco and the beach. It is designed to leave you few practical reasons to go anywhere else.

THE MAMETAS VERDICT

Spectacular, very expensive and distinctive enough that your money seems to have bought more than beige marble.

Why we keep it

The architecture and view are the main product here. If neither moves you, there are considerably cheaper ways to sleep near Monaco.

The shuttle to Monaco and the beach also solves the main inconvenience of a hotel suspended on a cliff: the cliff.

Who it suits

  • You want the hotel itself to be a destination.
  • Architecture, spa and views matter a lot to you.
  • You want Monaco close by without actually sleeping there.

Look elsewhere if...

  • You like stepping outside and improvising on foot.
  • Budget is one of your deciding factors.
  • You prefer old stone to highly contemporary lines.
