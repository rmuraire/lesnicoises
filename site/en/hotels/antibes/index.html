Where to stay in Antibes and Juan-les-Pins?

Eleven addresses, three moods. Practical old-town Antibes, lively Juan-les-Pins or a more polished Riviera stay: choose the atmosphere you want outside the hotel first.

Decide before booking

Antibes and Juan-les-Pins have separate stations and different holiday rhythms. That is exactly why one flat list was a bad idea.

The shortlist is deliberately short. The point is not to recreate Booking with fewer filters, but to reduce the choice to a few understandable ways of travelling.
