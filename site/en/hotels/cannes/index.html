Choose the stay before the room

Where to stay in Cannes?

Fifteen hotels, three ways to do Cannes. Palace, central luxury or something more intimate: the wrong choice gets expensive quickly here, in money and patience.

Decide before booking

For a festival or congress, distance to the Palais matters. On holiday, the question is simpler: do you want Cannes as spectacle, as base or as backdrop?

The shortlist is deliberately short. The point is not to recreate Booking with fewer filters, but to reduce the choice to a few understandable ways of travelling.
