Boscolo Nice Hôtel & Spa

A proper five-star city hotel: spa, rooftop, two pools and enough marble to remind you what bracket you booked.

Boscolo Nice is a five-star Belle Époque property on boulevard Victor Hugo. The hotel lists 112 rooms, a large spa with indoor pool, a rooftop pool and dining, while the Nice tourism office lists the property as Nice Rainbow.

Unlike a Promenade palace, this is more of a full-service city hotel: central, polished and built for people who actually intend to use the hotel between outings.

AreaBoulevard Victor Hugo / centre
Format112 rooms

THE MAMETAS VERDICT

Book it when the hotel itself needs to earn part of the holiday. If you only need a bed in central Nice, this is a rather expensive way to get one.

Who it suits

  • You want spa and rooftop time built into the stay.
  • You want five-star service without being directly on the Promenade.
  • You want a Nice Rainbow luxury option in the city centre.

Look elsewhere if...

  • You mainly want to be in Old Nice.
  • You will spend every waking hour outside the hotel.
  • Your budget does not enjoy dramatic statements.
