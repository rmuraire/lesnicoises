Hôtel Villa Victoria

Central Nice with a softer rhythm: a good answer when calm matters but exile does not.

Who it suits

Villa Victoria works best for travellers who value a garden, a calmer street and a central location without needing to be on the Promenade. The garden and residential feel suit people who want to come back to something quieter after the day.

Mèfi: the trade-off

You remain close enough to the centre to walk most evenings, without buying into the idea that every good Nice hotel must face the sea and charge accordingly.
