Hôtel Le Grimaldi by HappyCulture

Central without feeling corporate. Colour, old-Nice charm and a quieter address in the Carré d’Or.

Le Grimaldi is a 47-room four-star in central Nice, a few minutes from the Promenade and pedestrian streets. Its official site leans into colourful Mediterranean interiors rather than anonymous business-hotel beige.

The useful thing is the location: central enough for a first stay, close enough to the sea, and calmer than sleeping directly in Old Nice or on the busiest part of the Promenade.

THE MAMETAS VERDICT

A sensible middle ground: enough personality to feel like Nice, central enough to make getting around boring, which is exactly what you want.

Who it suits

  • You want the Carré d’Or rather than the station or Old Nice.
  • You prefer a smaller four-star to a large seafront property.
  • You want Nice Rainbow certification without making it the only reason to book.

Mèfi: watch out if...

  • You want a pool, spa or resort-scale facilities.
  • A sea view is non-negotiable.
  • You want the nightlife literally outside the lobby.
