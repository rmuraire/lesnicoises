French Riviera hotels with a private beach

Eighteen hotels that genuinely have their own beach or beach club, from Nice to Saint-Tropez. Checked one by one, with what to know before booking.

Mametas CheckedAmenities checked · 12 October 2026
Private beachRun by the hotel or its group, for guests or with priority access.
Sand or pebblesSand in Antibes, Juan-les-Pins, Cannes and Pampelonne; pebbles in Nice.
In seasonMost beaches open from April-May to September-October.
THE MAMETAS VERDICT

For sand with no shuttle, choose Antibes, Juan-les-Pins or Cannes. In Nice the private beach is on pebbles; in Saint-Tropez it is on Pampelonne, not in front of the hotel; in Monaco the Beach Club is out of town.
