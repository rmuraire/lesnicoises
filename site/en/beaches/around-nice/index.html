BEACHES · AROUND NICE

Beaches around Nice that are worth the trip

Yes, some beaches around Nice are prettier. Nissa will recover. So will you, assuming your calves survive.

Mametas CheckedPractical information checked · August 2026

A better beach may require twenty minutes on a train, a bus or some stairs. Mèfi (watch out): if your “hidden gem” has 1.8 million views on Instagram, pichoun, you are no longer an explorer. You are following a queue.

Mèfi: watch out for THE “SECRET BEACH”

No car: Marinières or Baie des Fourmis. Scenery: Petite Afrique or Mala. Quieter: Fossettes. Late sun: Passable. And if you demand dégun around at 3 pm in August, we can no longer help you.

VILLEFRANCHE-SUR-MER

The beaches, one by one

Plage des Marinières

The easiest from Nice.

The largest beach in Villefranche, right inside the bay and easy to reach by regional train. Lifeguarded in summer. Considerably simpler than the scenery suggests.

AccessTER regional train from Nice; the beach runs directly along Villefranche bay.
ServicesLifeguarded in summer. Easy for a no-car half day.

BEAULIEU-SUR-MER

Baie des Fourmis

For stepping off the train and being almost there.

A gravel beach facing Villa Kérylos, less than 500 metres from the station and supervised June to August.

AccessBeaulieu-sur-Mer station is under 500 m away.
TypeGravel beach facing Villa Kérylos; supervised June to August.

Google Maps ↗︎

BEAULIEU-SUR-MER

Petite Afrique

For pine trees and a proper beach day.

East of the port beneath the cliffs, with a children’s play area, beach volleyball, watersports and seasonal private beach clubs. Supervised June to August. More equipped than secret, which may be exactly what you need.

Good forEast of the port beneath the cliffs, built for a proper beach day.
ServicesPlay area, beach volleyball, watersports and seasonal private clubs; supervised June to August.

Google Maps ↗︎

SAINT-JEAN-CAP-FERRAT

Les Fossettes

For the quieter side of the peninsula.

The tourist office describes it as one of the most unspoilt and discreet beaches on Cap-Ferrat, appreciated for its underwater life. Small and not wheelchair accessible.

FeelSmall and unspoilt, particularly valued for its underwater life.
AccessibilityNot listed as wheelchair accessible.

Google Maps ↗︎

SAINT-JEAN-CAP-FERRAT

Passable

For afternoon sun and the view across Villefranche bay.

On the western side of the peninsula, open all year with both public and concession areas. Its exposure keeps the sun later into the afternoon, a detail that becomes surprisingly important around 5:30 pm.

ExposureWest side of Cap-Ferrat: the sun stays later into the afternoon.
StatusPublic and concession areas; open all year.

Google Maps ↗︎

CAP-D’AIL

Plage Mala

The dramatic one. It charges partly in stairs.

A cove below the cliffs with fine sand and small pebbles, reached by the coastal path or the Mala steps. The setting is magnificent. Your calves will also remember it. Hu! Beauty never promised a lift.

AccessVia the coastal path or the Mala steps. The scenery charges partly in calves.
TypeA cliff-backed cove with fine sand and small pebbles.
