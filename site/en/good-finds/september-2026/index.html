RIVIERA AGENDA · SEPTEMBER 2026

September on the Riviera

August is packing away its beach towels. The Riviera is not closing. Here is what really deserves a place on your radar in September, and what may simply make getting around harder.

MAMETAS SAYS

September may be one of the best months on the Riviera: summer is still here, the crowds ease a little, and enough is happening to make the coast feel properly alive.

Past · 5 September, Festa de la Sant Bertoumiéu, Nice

A traditional festival on the Promenade du Paillon, listed by the City of Nice from 10am to 11.30pm, celebrating local food, crafts and inland traditions. If you want something more Niçois than a rooftop DJ imported from Paris, start here.

Practical. City of Nice ↗ · Google Maps ↗

Past · 8-13 September, Cannes Yachting Festival

The show takes over the Vieux Port and Port Canto, with more than 700 boats announced and a late opening until 10pm on Friday 11 September. If boats are your thing, go. If they are not, at least remember that Cannes will be considerably busier than usual.

Practical. Official source ↗ · Google Maps ↗ · Google Maps ↗

Past · 12-13 September, IRONMAN 70.3 World Championship, Nice

The useful tip here is simply to know before you arrive. The City of Nice has published extensive road and parking restrictions for the week, with major closures around the Promenade des Anglais, Quai des États-Unis and airport approaches on 12 and 13 September. Jardin Albert 1er and parts of the Promenade du Paillon are also due to close from 2 to 16 September. Mèfi (watch out) if you have a flight or a dinner described as “ten minutes by taxi”.

Practical. City of Nice restrictions ↗ · Google Maps ↗

All month, Ellsworth Kelly and Peter Knapp at Fondation Maeght

Two very good reasons to go up to Saint-Paul-de-Vence. Ellsworth Kelly, At the Water’s Edge runs to 15 November, while Peter Knapp: The Era of Courrèges runs to 8 November. One important detail: Fondation Maeght lists an exceptional closure on 5, 6 and 7 September.

Practical. Ellsworth Kelly ↗ · Peter Knapp ↗ · Google Maps ↗

19-20 September, European Heritage Days

The 43rd edition takes place on 19 and 20 September, with special openings and programmes across France. On the Riviera, the Fernand Léger National Museum in Biot, for example, is offering guided visits, family workshops and free admission over the weekend. Check the programme shortly before you go: this is exactly when less obvious places can become the best ones.

Practical. Official programme ↗ · Google Maps ↗

23-26 September, Monaco Yacht Show

Port Hercule goes into superyacht mode for four days, from Wednesday 23 to Saturday 26 September. Visitor passes are expensive and Wednesday access is more restricted, so this is not really a casual “we happened to be nearby” visit. Even if you do not attend, expect Monaco to be busy.

Practical. Official source ↗ · Google Maps ↗

Our pick

Right now: Fondation Maeght on a weekday, lunch in Saint-Paul-de-Vence, then back towards the sea. Monaco Yacht Show only if yachting is really the point of the day; otherwise give Port Hercule a little breathing room until the event ends. The Mediterranean is still there.
