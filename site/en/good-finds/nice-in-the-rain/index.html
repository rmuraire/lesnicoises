PRACTICAL · NICE IN THE RAIN

What to do in Nice when it rains

It is raining. Hu. You paid for the Côte d’Azur and the sky has ignored your booking. What a mistake.

MAMACClosed for renovation. Rain does not create a special exemption.
6 museumsEnough to rescue a day. You do not need to visit all six. This is not an exam.
TuesdaySeveral Nice museums close on Tuesdays. The weather did not coordinate its schedule.

Yes, it rains in Nice. Do not look so betrayed, pichoun. Dégun sold you 365 days of blue sky. The city has had centuries to prepare, and several of its best museums can save the day without you sulking in a shopping centre.

ISSA, NOBODY DIED

Matisse or Chagall in the morning, lunch in town, Lascaris or photography in the afternoon, then a drink. There. Three drops of rain are not going to ruin your holiday.

MORNINGMatisse or Chagall

Give one major museum proper time rather than collecting entrances.

LUNCHBack to the centre

Old Nice or the Carré d’Or keeps the day walkable.

AFTERNOONLascaris or photography

Compact, central and easy to combine without turning rain into admin.

1. Matisse Museum

In Cimiez, in a Genoese villa surrounded by olive trees. The exhibition Henri Matisse - Yves Saint Laurent. Beauty, Fashion and Happiness runs ended 28 September 2026. The museum opens 10 am to 6 pm until the end of October, closed Tuesdays. Mèfi: watch out: discovering that last detail at the locked door, looking thoroughly empégué, does not improve the weather.

Cimiez · allow 60-90 min. Map ↗ · Official ↗

2. Marc Chagall National Museum

Built around Chagall’s Biblical Message cycle and an exceptional collection of his work. In 2026 it opens 10 am to 1 pm and 2:30 pm to 6 pm from May to October, closed Tuesdays.

Cimiez edge · allow 60-90 min. Map ↗ · Official ↗

3. Palais Lascaris

A 17th-century Baroque palace in Old Nice with decorated salons and a historic musical instrument collection. Compact enough to be useful when the rain becomes ambitious.

Old Nice · allow 45-60 min. Map ↗ · Official ↗

4. Charles Nègre Photography Museum

Beside Cours Saleya. Small enough to combine with lunch, serious enough to justify the visit. The Mathieu Forget exhibition runs ended 27 September 2026. Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10 am to 6 pm.

Cours Saleya · allow 45-75 min. Map ↗ · Official ↗

5. Villa Masséna

On the Promenade des Anglais, covering the history of Nice and the Riviera inside a Belle Époque mansion. Open 10 am to 6 pm until the end of October, closed Tuesdays.

Promenade · allow 60-90 min. Map ↗ · Official ↗

6. Jules Chéret Fine Arts Museum

A little away from the obvious centre, in a 19th-century villa with collections spanning the 16th to 20th centuries. Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10 am to 6 pm until the end of October 2026.

Baumettes · allow 60-90 min. Map ↗ · Official ↗

And MAMAC?

Closed since January 2024 for major renovation connected with the Promenade du Paillon extension. The work will take several years. If an old blog sends you there, Mèfi: watch out. Whoever wrote it may also have excellent advice on fax machines.
