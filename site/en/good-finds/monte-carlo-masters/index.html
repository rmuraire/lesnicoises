Rolex Monte-Carlo Masters 2027: dates, tickets, getting there and where to stay

The Riviera’s big clay-court week, played in a club called Monte-Carlo that sits in France. What is on sale, which court to choose, and why Menton is the smartest place to sleep.

3-11 April 2027Qualifying on 3 and 4 April, final on Sunday 11 April.
Tickets on saleSince 15 October 2026, on the official shop only.
In FranceThe Monte-Carlo Country Club is in Roquebrune-Cap-Martin, on the Monaco border.
THE MAMETAS VERDICT

Sleep in Menton, come by train. In 2025 and 2026 a temporary station opened a few hundred metres from the club, served only by trains coming from the Menton side. Its return in 2027 is not confirmed yet. From Nice, count on the train to Monaco, then a bus.

What is confirmed

The 2027 tournament runs from Saturday 3 to Sunday 11 April. Qualifying is on 3 and 4 April, the first round also starts on the Sunday, and the final is on Sunday 11 April: doubles at noon, singles at 3pm. Play usually starts at 11am and gates open at 9.30am.

Tickets

General sale opened on 15 October 2026, after a priority sale for 2026 buyers. Everything goes through the official shop: day tickets, multi-day passes, hospitality packages and the Grande Nuit du Tennis. Tickets are sold court by court: Court Rainier III, the centre court, Court des Princes or Court E-A de Massy. Check which court yours covers before the day.

From €37Cheapest 2027 day ticket, on the first qualifying Saturday.
From €115Entry price on the semi-final and final weekend, 10 and 11 April.
PassesAll four 2027 passes showed as sold out on 26 October.

If you cannot go, resell through the official shop: tickets bought on unofficial sites may be refused at the gate. Children under five go free on an adult’s lap; that adult must show ID.

Getting there without a car

From Menton: about ten minutes by regional train. In 2026 the temporary Monte-Carlo Country Club stop, about 500-600 metres from Entrance 4, was served in one direction only, for spectators coming from the Menton side, and was not wheelchair accessible. Trains back towards Menton do not stop there: return via Monaco-Monte-Carlo station or the Zou! line 600 bus.

From Nice: the TER (regional train) reaches Monaco-Monte-Carlo in about 23 minutes. From there, take a Monaco bus: the 2026 access guide listed lines 1 and 4 to Saint-Roman, and lines 5 and 6 to Larvotto. The whole Monaco bus network was also free from Monday 6 to Friday 10 April 2026. The Zou! line 600 bus (Nice-Monaco-Menton) also stops at Saint-Roman.

By car: the recommended car park in 2026 was Les Salines in Monaco, €6 a day, with a free shuttle. The rest of the area is tight. Leaving the Monaco side by ride-hailing car has its own rules: see taxis, ride-hailing and parking.

On the day

Four entrances, a security search at each, and no re-entry once you leave. No suitcases or large bags: paid lockers at Entrances 2, 3 and 4. You may bring your own food. Animals are not allowed.

Where to stay

MentonTen minutes by train, at Menton prices.
RoquebruneThe club is on its territory, with few hotels and high-end ones.
MonacoA bus ride away, at Monaco prices.
NiceTrain plus bus, and the whole city in the evening.
