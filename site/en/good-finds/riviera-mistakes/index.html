The Mametas Have Opinions.

Five women. A lifetime on the Riviera. And absolutely no reason to keep quiet.

Ginette, Lulu, Mireille, Odette and Rosine have spent decades on the Promenade des Anglais, watching the world arrive in the wrong shoes. They are fictional. Their opinions are not.

Monaco isn’t trying to impress you. It doesn’t know you’re there.

Mireille

A secret spot with its own hashtag is called a waiting room.

Odette

Three hotels in five days? You’re not visiting the Riviera. You’re auditioning mattresses.

Rosine

My second husband proposed on the Croisette. My third had the decency to do it in Menton, where nobody was watching.

Lulu

Sunglasses here are not an accessory. They’re a way of not saying hello.

Lulu

The Riviera does not disappoint. August does.

Odette

A tourist once asked me where the real Nice was. I was standing in it, buying tomatoes.

Odette

The table has a sea view. The food prefers not to be seen.

Rosine

A convertible on the Riviera: the best seat in the traffic jam.

Lulu

In 1962, a man followed me from the Negresco to the port to ask my name. Today they follow me with a phone to ask for directions. Progress.

Ginette

At 18 degrees, Niçois put on a coat and tourists go swimming. That’s how we tell you apart.

Ginette

Those are pebbles, darling. Not a customer-service issue.

Ginette

Your itinerary has twelve stops. Is there a prize at the end?

Rosine

I once had lunch in Saint-Tropez with a man who owned three yachts. He talked about all three. I took the bus home.

Mireille

Luxury is an empty beach in October. Everything else is just expensive.

Mireille

Elegance is knowing when to leave Saint-Tropez. Ideally before arriving.

Mireille
