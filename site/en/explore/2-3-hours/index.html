EXPLORE · 2-3 HOURS

Only got 2 or 3 hours? Good.

A short window is not a failed half-day; it is often the best antidote to an overpacked itinerary. These eight routes actually fit the time advertised.

8 routesNo car by defaultReal time, not brochure time
2h30

Nice

ROUTE · Cours Saleya → Old Nice → Castle Hill → Port

Start around Cours Saleya, cross Old Nice, climb Castle Hill, descend toward the Port. If you only have 2 hours, cut the Port.

BEST FOR
First look at Nice

THE CATCH
The climb up Castle Hill, though the lift may be running depending on opening times.

Official source →

3h

Cannes

ROUTE · Forville → Le Suquet → Place de la Castre → Old Port → Croisette

Catch the market in the morning if it is open, then do the old town before finishing at the port and Croisette. It is more interesting in that order.

BEST FOR
Short first visit

THE CATCH
Forville is currently Tue-Sun 7am-1pm; otherwise start at Le Suquet.

Official source →

2h30

Antibes

ROUTE · Station → old town → Picasso Museum → ramparts → Gravette

If you go inside the Picasso Museum, make it the real centre of the slot. If you mainly want the walk, stay outside and keep the ramparts + Gravette.

BEST FOR
Old town + culture

THE CATCH
The museum turns this into a culture visit. Do not go in just out of habit.

Official source →

2h

Villefranche-sur-Mer

ROUTE · Station → old town → Rue Obscure → Citadel → harbour → Marinières

A compact loop through lanes, heritage and water. Do not build the slot around the Citadel museum: its museum rooms are currently closed for renovation.

BEST FOR
Beauty without a full day

THE CATCH
Steps and light-to-moderate gradients.

Official source →

2h30

Menton

ROUTE · Biovès → old town → Saint-Michel → Sablettes

Climb gradually to Saint-Michel, take in the view, then come down toward the sea. With 30 minutes more, extend toward the Bastion/Cocteau.

BEST FOR
Colour + old town

THE CATCH
The climb to Saint-Michel is the only real effort.

Official source →

2h30

Monaco

ROUTE · Place d’Armes → Rock → Palace → Cathedral → Saint-Martin gardens → Oceanographic Museum exterior

The Rock fits 2-3 hours if you stay outside. If you enter the Oceanographic Museum, make that the main event: the museum itself recommends about 2 hours.

BEST FOR
Historic Monaco

THE CATCH
Do not combine the Rock + full museum + Casino in the same slot.

Official source →

2h

Saint-Paul-de-Vence

ROUTE · Place de Gaulle → Rue Grande → ramparts → cemetery → village

Two hours works for the village if you do not add Fondation Maeght. If Maeght is the priority, give it the slot: the Saint-Paul tourist office gives an average visit of about 90 minutes, closer to 2 hours if you linger in the gardens.

BEST FOR
Village + atmosphere

THE CATCH
Village + Fondation in 2 hours is a bad plan.

Official source →

2-3h

Èze

ROUTE · Village → lanes → garden / viewpoint → church

If you arrive at the village, two hours is enough for a real visit. From Èze-sur-Mer, the Nietzsche path is 2.1 km with roughly 350-400 m ascent and takes about 1-1.5 hours just to climb.

BEST FOR
Perched village

THE CATCH
The Nietzsche path eats up much of the slot. It is not a casual way up.

Official source →
