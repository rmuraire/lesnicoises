easyJetCurrently allows travel through end of week 35 for singleton pregnancies / week 32 for multiples if uncomplicated.

When to come

If dates are flexible, late May, June or September usually make the same coastline easier to live with than July and August.

The low-friction outing worth knowing

The Promenade Maurice Rouvier between Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat and Beaulieu is 1.3 km one way, about 20 minutes, fully tarmacked and stair-free. It is a much better fit for this trip than turning a coastal walk into a hike.

Build days around energy, not ambition

  • One main outing per day is enough.
  • Keep the hotel within easy reach at midday.
  • Front-load walking before lunch in hot months.
  • Use trains for Antibes, Cannes, Monaco and Menton.
  • Keep one day with no destination at all.

Food, filtered for pregnancy

Freshly cooked socca, well-cooked farcis and cooked pissaladière are straightforward choices. French health guidance advises avoiding raw-milk soft cheeses, raw or undercooked meat, raw fish and shellfish, and taking care with raw produce where toxoplasmosis is a concern.

Babymoon FAQ

Is the French Riviera good for a babymoon?

Yes, if you plan for comfort rather than coverage. Nice has the best transport and healthcare backup; Beaulieu is calmer and flatter for a stay with few decisions to make.

Can I fly to Nice at 30 weeks pregnant?

Airline rules differ. NHS guidance says airlines may request a doctor or midwife letter after week 28. British Airways requires documentation from week 28, while easyJet currently allows uncomplicated singleton pregnancies through the end of week 35 without a certificate.

Where are pregnancy emergencies handled in Nice?

CHU Nice lists 24/7 gynaecological and obstetric emergency care at Hôpital Archet 2, 151 Route Saint-Antoine de Ginestière.

Is July too hot for a babymoon?

It can be uncomfortable, especially in an older apartment without strong air conditioning. For flexibility, late May, June and September are easier choices than peak July–August.

What local food needs extra attention?

French health guidance advises pregnant travellers to avoid raw-milk soft cheeses, raw or undercooked meat, raw fish and shellfish, and to wash raw fruit and vegetables carefully where toxoplasmosis is a concern.

How do I find an out-of-hours pharmacy?

3237.fr and the 3237 phone service provide pharmacy-on-duty information in covered areas; for urgent medical advice in France call 15, or 112 for the European emergency number.
