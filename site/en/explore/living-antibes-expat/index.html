LIVING HERE · ANTIBES

Antibes is not just where expats end up. There are reasons.

Sea, trains, Sophia Antipolis, yachting and a year-round town explain the international pull. The useful question is whether its daily compromises fit your version of Riviera life.

  • What is the DPE rating? G-rated homes can no longer be rented.
  • Is this genuinely a 12-month furnished lease?
  • Do not assume you can short-let it later: nationwide registration rules changed in 2026 and local rules still apply.

Foreign job, French address

Do not treat a foreign payroll as an immigration or social-security solution. Visa route, tax residence and social-security position are separate files. A visitor visa is not a work visa.

Living in Antibes FAQ

How much is rent in Antibes?

Le Figaro’s August 2026 estimate put median apartment rent at €25/m² overall, €24/m² for 2-room apartments and €22/m² for 3-room apartments. Treat this as a market indicator.

How long is Antibes to Monaco by train?

SNCF listed 57 direct trains a day on 2 October 2026, with an average journey of 55 minutes and the fastest around 50 minutes.

How long is Antibes to Nice Airport by train?

Direct TER services from Antibes reach Nice Saint-Augustin in roughly 16–27 minutes on current timetables; the airport tram connection is at Grand Arénas / Saint-Augustin.

Do I need a car if I work in Sophia Antipolis?

Not necessarily. CASA’s Line B links central Antibes to Sophia every 12–15 minutes and is free with the Pass Navette, but the exact office and home address still determine whether the commute is easy.

What are international school fees near Antibes?

Published 2026–27 tuition ranges checked include about €15,960–25,600 at Mougins British International School and €12,780–16,995 at ICS Côte d’Azur; ASEICA’s public international sections use a much lower differentiated fee scale.

Is Antibes an expat bubble?

No. Sophia tech, Port Vauban yachting, tourism and long-term residents overlap, but they are not one community and they do not live on the same geography.

Can I live in France and keep working remotely for my foreign employer?

Possibly, but not by assumption. Your immigration route, tax residence and social-security position must all fit. A visitor visa is not a work visa, and when French social-security law applies a foreign employer can have French registration and contribution obligations.
