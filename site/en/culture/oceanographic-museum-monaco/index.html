ART & CULTURE · MONACO

Musée océanographique de Monaco

You can of course spend Monaco counting Ferraris. Or walk into a museum built into the cliff. Different ambitions.

Mametas CheckedPractical information rechecked 12 October 2026
MAMETAS SAYS

Not an art museum, but culturally it belongs here. The building is spectacular, the aquariums are substantial and Monaco’s oceanographic history gives the Rock more depth.

Why we go

Opened in 1910, the museum combines historic collections, marine science, aquariums and exhibitions. Its almost impossible position above the Mediterranean is part of the experience.

What to actually look at

The aquariums, obviously, but also the historic halls and architecture. The museum suggests around two hours. For once, do not try to “do Monaco” in 47 minutes.

THE PRACTICAL BIT THAT CHANGES THE DAYChecked 12 October 2026
AddressAvenue Saint-Martin, 98000 Monaco
Getting thereTER to Monaco-Monte-Carlo, then about 20 minutes on foot with the right station exit, or buses 1 / 2 to Monaco-Ville. Pêcheurs car park sits below the museum.
Time neededAbout 2 hours, the museum’s own recommended visit time.
When to goEvery day except 25 December and Grand Prix weekend. At opening time, before the groups.
Pair it withThe Rock: the Prince’s Palace and old town, on foot. Monaco guide.
Hours & priceOctober to March: daily 10am-6pm. 10am-7pm from April to June and in September, 9.30am-8pm in July-August. Closed 25 December and Grand Prix weekend. Full price €22.50; €14 for ages 4 to 17; free under 4. Last entry 30 minutes before closing
BookingOnline ticketing is available. Last admission is 30 minutes before closing.
