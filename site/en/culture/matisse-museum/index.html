Musée Matisse

Matisse lived in Nice for nearly forty years. You may continue believing southern light is just a hashtag, but that would be a pity.

Mametas CheckedPractical information rechecked 12 October 2026
MAMETAS SAYS

Less spectacular than some Riviera foundations, but more deeply tied to Nice. This is where the long relationship between Matisse, the city, colour and light starts making sense.

Why we go

Set in a Genoese villa in Cimiez, the museum brings together paintings, drawings, sculpture, cut-outs and working material. The nearby Régina, where Matisse lived, and the wider Cimiez setting give the collection context.

What to actually look at

Do not hunt only for the famous works. Look at the drawings, models and working stages. Henri Matisse / Yves Saint Laurent, which ended on 28 September 2026, created a very natural conversation with fashion.

Matisse is buried in the Cimiez Monastery cemetery, a few minutes’ walk from the museum. Go after your visit, through the olive grove: it is free, quiet and ends the visit better than the gift shop.

Address164 avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice
Getting thereFrom central Nice: use the Lignes d’Azur bus network to Cimiez. The train is not useful for this visit.
Time needed1.5 to 2 hours. Add time if you stay around the Roman arena and Cimiez gardens.
When to goClosed until 20 October 2026, then the Matisse-Alex Katz exhibition from 28 October to 18 January. Closed on Tuesdays.
Pair it withThe Cimiez arena and monastery next door, then Old Nice.
Hours & priceClosed until 20 October 2026. Then 10am-6pm until 31 October, 10am-5pm from 1 November to 31 March; closed on Tuesdays. Full price €10, €12 during the Matisse-Alex Katz exhibition (28 October-18 January). Ticket desk closes 30 minutes before the museum
BookingOnline tickets are useful on busy days. Guided visits have their own booking rules.
