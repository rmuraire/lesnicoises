ART & CULTURE · MENTON

Musée Jean Cocteau, Le Bastion

The big Ricciotti museum is closed. The Bastion, meanwhile, keeps doing the job without taking up an entire block.

MAMETAS SAYS

Small, very personal and directly connected with Cocteau, who shaped the place, the mosaics and the display himself. You do not need three hours for a visit to count.

Why we go

Cocteau discovered the 17th-century fort in the late 1950s and agreed to turn it into a home for his work. He followed the restoration, designed mosaics and shaped the interior. The original collection held 102 works and was later expanded through donations.

What to actually look at

Look at the building as much as the art. In 2026, Le château de la Bête runs from 4 July to 16 November. And Mèfi (watch out): the larger Jean Cocteau Collection Séverin Wunderman building remains closed for technical reasons.
