ART & CULTURE · VILLEFRANCHE-SUR-MER

Saint-Pierre Chapel by Cocteau

Small fishermen’s chapel, large artist ego, surprisingly right result. Cocteau left rather more than a signature here.

Mametas CheckedPractical information rechecked 23 September 2026 · official source
MAMETAS SAYS

A short visit that immediately improves a day in Villefranche. Do not cross the Riviera for it alone; if you are already on the harbour, go in.

Why we go

Jean Cocteau decorated the fishermen’s chapel of Saint-Pierre in 1956. The scenes mix Mediterranean life with episodes from Saint Peter’s story in an instantly recognisable graphic language.

What to actually look at

The five main painted scenes and the way they wrap around the small interior. The strength comes less from quantity than from the fit between the chapel, the harbour and the images.

PRACTICAL
Address4 Quai Courbet, on Villefranche harbour
Time needed10-20 minutes
Hours & priceIn 2026 the chapel is open Wednesday-Sunday, 9:30 am-12:30 pm and 2-6 pm, with annual closure from mid-November to mid-December. Adult entry €4; under 15s free. Not wheelchair accessible according to the official listing

Do it on a Villefranche-Cap-Ferrat day. Ten to twenty minutes may be enough, but they have more personality than many hour-long museum visits.
