Art and culture in Nice: 6 museums worth your time

You can put the iPhone down for two hours and walk into a museum. Dégun will revoke your holiday status.

Nice has enough museums to offer something beyond tanning. If culture is not your thing, go to the beach, we will survive. But do not tell us Nice has “no culture” after seeing three beach clubs and the Chanel boutique.

Mèfi: watch out, NO NEED TO TICK EVERY BOX

Matisse for Nice and colour. Chagall for a unique ensemble. Lascaris for the building. After that, choose what interests you. Six museums in five hours impresses dégun and you will have looked at nothing.

CIMIEZ

The museums

Matisse Museum

The one most naturally tied to Nice.

Matisse lived in Nice for nearly four decades. The museum in Cimiez helps explain why the local light became almost a material in his work. Closed until 20 October 2026 inclusive; the exhibition “Henri Matisse - Alex Katz. The Silence of Painting” opens on 28 October and runs until 18 January 2027.

AVENUE DOCTEUR MÉNARD

Marc Chagall National Museum

For the Biblical Message, and more.

Designed around Chagall’s Biblical Message cycle, with paintings, mosaics and stained glass that really feel part of one project.

15 RUE DROITE

Palais Lascaris

For Baroque architecture in the middle of Old Nice.

Decorated salons, painted ceilings, staircases and historic instruments. Even visitors with limited patience for museum labels tend to find something worth looking at.

1 PLACE PIERRE GAUTIER

Charles Nègre Photography Museum

For its exhibitions and a pleasantly compact visit.

Forty-five minutes can be enough, which is a compliment. On show: “Youkine Lefèvre - Lone Islands” until 8 November, and “Sabine Weiss - Une vie de photographe” from 16 October 2026 to 31 January 2027. Closed on Mondays.

65 RUE DE FRANCE

Villa Masséna

For Nice before Instagram.

Local and Riviera history inside a Belle Époque villa on the Promenade. The gardens are free and the museum puts some history behind the facades. Nice did, in fact, exist before your phone arrived. Hu! Even before seventeen-second Reels with Italian music.

33 AVENUE DES BAUMETTES

Jules Chéret Fine Arts Museum

For something beyond the three names everyone knows.

Collections from the 16th to 20th centuries in a 19th-century villa. Quieter, less central and often better for exactly those reasons.

The notable absence: MAMAC

The modern and contemporary art museum remains closed for major renovation. Its teams continue off-site projects, but the building is not open to visitors. Old travel lists have proved considerably more durable than museum opening hours. Mèfi: watch out for the ficanas copying the same “must-sees” without checking whether the door still opens.
