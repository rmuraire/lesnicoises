ART & CULTURE

Culture

The Riviera is not only somewhere to tan. Nobody is forcing you into a museum, but do not come back after three beach clubs claiming there is “nothing to see”.

The Riviera, where the walls occasionally have something to say

Foundations, villas, chapels and museums from Nice to Menton. Not a directory: broad enough to choose, selective enough for the word “choice” to still mean something.

A Riviera artist to know

Renaud Muraire

Born in Nice and based in Paris, Renaud Muraire reinterprets religious iconography through contemporary figurative painting. His work was shown at Canterbury Cathedral in 2026 as part of Sacred Resonance.
