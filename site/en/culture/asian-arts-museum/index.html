Asian Arts Museum

A free Kenzo Tange museum set on water near the airport. Nice occasionally has very efficient ways to fill an awkward final hour.

Mametas CheckedPractical information rechecked 12 October 2026
MAMETAS SAYS

Not a first-weekend essential. A very good choice, though, if you like architecture, have already seen the classics or have an inconvenient gap before a flight.

Why we go

The museum presents Asian art collections inside a building designed by Kenzo Tange. The interplay of square and circle, white marble and water gives the place a very distinctive presence.

What to actually look at

Start with the building, then move through the collections by cultural region. Its position beside Parc Phoenix and the airport makes it unusually practical as well.

PRACTICAL
Address405 Promenade des Anglais, by Parc Phoenix
Getting thereTram T2, stop Parc Phénix. This is one of the rare museums that actually works before or after an airport trip
Time needed45 minutes to 1 hour
When to goClosed on Tuesdays. Right before or after a flight: the museum is next to the airport.
Pair it withParc Phoenix next door, then the Promenade and Nice beaches.
Hours & priceOpen Wednesday to Monday, 10am-5pm from September to June and 10am-6pm in July-August; closed on Tuesdays. Free entry. “Mortimer et le Japon de papier” exhibition until 31 January 2027
