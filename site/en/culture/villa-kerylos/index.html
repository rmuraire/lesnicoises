ART & CULTURE · BEAULIEU-SUR-MER

Villa Kérylos

An archaeological recreation by the sea could have turned into a theme park for classicists. Mèfi: watch out, it is much more elegant than that.

Mametas CheckedPractical information rechecked 12 October 2026
MAMETAS SAYS

Kérylos is one of those places that makes the Riviera wonderfully absurd: a reimagined Greek villa completed in 1908, sitting above the Baie des Fourmis.

Why we go

Théodore Reinach and architect Emmanuel Pontremoli wanted to recreate a Hellenistic Greek house without giving up early-20th-century comfort. Marble, mosaics, frescoes and custom furniture make the project obsessive, which is precisely why it works.

What to actually look at

The peristyle, andron, mosaics and sea views. Give the rooms some time. Just because everything is photogenic does not mean you need to turn the visit into a property shoot.

When to goEvery day; from October to March it closes at 5pm.
Pair it withVilla Ephrussi, then the Promenade Maurice-Rouvier to Saint-Jean, a half-hour walk.
Hours & priceOctober to March: daily 10am-5pm; April to September: 10am-6pm. Closed 1 January, 1 May, 1 and 11 November and 25 December. Full price €15
BookingTicketing is available. A combined ticket with Villa Ephrussi is offered.
