FRENCH RIVIERA · LGBTQ+ TRAVEL

Gay French Riviera: the useful version

The useful question is not “where is the gay Riviera?” It is “where should I base myself, and how do I use the coast without turning the whole trip into LGBTQ+ logistics?”

Best single base: NiceStrong transport, visible LGBTQ+ ecosystem and the easiest access to both sides of the coast.
West: Antibes + CannesEasy from Nice; worth a separate base only if the western Riviera is the main event.
East: Monaco + MentonSimple train days. No need to invent a separate LGBTQ+ itinerary for every stop.
THE MAMETAS TAKE

For a first trip, sleep in Nice and travel out. Nice gives you the community layer when you want it without turning the whole holiday into LGBTQ+ logistics.

Gay French Riviera FAQ

Is Nice gay friendly?

Nice has the Riviera’s clearest LGBTQ+ visitor infrastructure, including the Nice Rainbow programme and labelled establishments.

Is there a gay beach in Nice?

The useful answer depends on access and current local use; see the dedicated no-car beach guide rather than assuming one beach works for every trip.

When is Nice Pride?

Event dates change. Check the current Nice / LGBTQ+ agenda before booking around Pride.

A note for queer women, trans and non-binary travellers

This guide currently has more concrete nightlife detail for gay men because that is where the dedicated venue offer we can verify is most visible. Do not read fewer venue cards as “nothing for you”. For current community events, mixed LGBTQIA+ nights and support, use Nice Côte d’Azur’s live LGBTQIA+ calendar and the Centre LGBTQIA+ Côte d’Azur; then use the ordinary Nice evening guide for the rest of the night. We would rather say where our coverage is strongest than pretend one men-only club represents everybody.
