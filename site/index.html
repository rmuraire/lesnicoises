Choose your base

Same coast. Different trip.

Choose the town before the hotel. Yes, we know, the pool photos are calling. Ignore them for five minutes. Your base decides whether the trip feels easy, lively, quiet or like a daily negotiation with a train timetable.

Entre nous · the Mametas

Three hotels in five days? Fada! Are you visiting the Riviera or testing mattresses? Pick the right base, use the train when it makes sense and unpack just once.

Best first base

Nice

It may not win every beauty contest. It keeps winning when you look at the train map.

Best for beauty

Villefranche & Cap-Ferrat

The bay does the seducing. The timetable asks for a little more attention.

Best balance

Antibes

Old town, proper beach, less theatre than Cannes.

Best for polish

Cannes

Beaches and hotels delivered with a straighter face.

Best for spectacle

Monaco

Palace, harbour and museums: very easy to visit, a much more particular choice as a base.

Best for a slower east

Menton

Gardens, colour and a softer eastern pace, with Italy almost within touching distance.

Once the base is settled, Hotel Fit cuts the list to a few addresses that match how you travel, with the reason and the catch.

Best for · atmosphere

Hôtel du Couvent

A remarkable hideaway in Old Nice when the hotel is part of the trip.

The catch: charm, slopes and heavy luggage are not always close friends.

Best for · smart logistics

Hôtel Apollinaire

Central, useful and considerably easier than organising every day around a glamorous sea view.

The catch: this is the practical choice.

Best for · romantic Nice

Hôtel La Pérouse

Above the sea at the edge of Old Nice, with the view doing a respectable amount of work.

The catch: the main railway station is not your neighbour.
