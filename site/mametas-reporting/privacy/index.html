Mametas Reporting privacy policy

This notice applies only to the private Mametas Reporting Google Apps Script, not to visits to the Mametas travel website. For visitor data and cookies, see the separate website privacy policy.

Data accessed

With the Google account owner's explicit OAuth authorisation, Mametas Reporting reads Google Analytics 4 aggregated performance metrics (sessions, traffic sources, engagement, landing pages and dates) and Google Search Console performance metrics (search queries, page URLs, impressions, clicks, click-through rates, average positions and dates).

The Google Sheets permission allows spreadsheet access and editing. The script is configured to write only to a specific private Mametas reporting spreadsheet, although the authorisation granted by Google covers spreadsheets more broadly. The script uses the Apps Script external request permission solely to call Google's Search Console API.

Why the data is used

The data support internal website performance measurement, content improvements and SEO decisions. They are not used to sell visitor information, identify individual visitors, create advertising audiences or run targeted advertising.

Processing, storage and sharing

The script requests data directly from Google Analytics and Search Console, and writes aggregated reports into the operator's private Google Sheets spreadsheet. Google Apps Script performs the daily refresh under the operator's Google authorisation. This utility does not send Google account access tokens to mametas.com or to a third-party marketing aggregator.

The spreadsheet is not part of the public website. Access to it is governed by the Google Drive sharing settings selected by the operator. Google processes requests, authorisations and execution logs under its own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

The six automated GA4 and Search Console reports are refreshed on a rolling 28-day basis and their prior contents are replaced on each successful update. Metrics remain in Google Sheets until replaced or deleted by the operator; Google may retain its own operational logs according to Google's policies. The operator can disable scheduled updates and delete the spreadsheet.

Revoking access

Authorisation can be withdrawn via the Google Account's security and connected-app settings. Revoking access prevents future API retrieval, but does not automatically delete reports already stored in the spreadsheet. The operator can delete those reports separately.

Contact

For information or deletion requests concerning this private utility, email mametas.riviera@gmail.com.
