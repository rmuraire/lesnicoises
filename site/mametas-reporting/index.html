Mametas Reporting

Mametas Reporting is a small private reporting tool for the operator of mametas.com. It is not a service for website visitors.

What this application does

The operator authorises a Google Apps Script to read aggregated Google Analytics 4 traffic statistics and Google Search Console search performance for Mametas. The script refreshes a privately controlled Google Sheets reporting spreadsheet every day. Its purpose is to understand organic traffic and improve the website's editorial content.

Reported metrics include sessions, acquisition channels, page visits, search queries, impressions, clicks, click-through rates and daily trends. The reports are not intended to identify individual website visitors.

Google account permissions

Google Analytics and Search Console permissions are read-only. Google Sheets permission is broader, but the actual script is programmed to update one specified reporting spreadsheet; it does not enumerate or modify other spreadsheets. The Google account owner controls access and can revoke authorisation at any time.

Privacy and security

Read the Mametas Reporting privacy policy for a description of the data accessed, why it is used, where it is stored, retention and how to revoke access. The separate Mametas website privacy policy governs visitor-facing cookies and analytics.

Terms of use

Access is reserved for the website operator and accounts personally authorised by the operator. The application is not offered as a public reporting service. Report outputs should be handled according to the privacy policy and applicable Google terms.
