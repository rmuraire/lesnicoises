Your data

Privacy & cookies

Mametas keeps optional tracking off until you choose. You can accept everything, reject everything or choose separately between audience measurement and smart affiliate tools. Refusing does not restrict the site.

Your optional choices

Audience measurement. Mametas uses Google Analytics in consent mode. Before you make a choice, Analytics storage is denied: no Analytics cookies are read or written, while Google may receive cookieless measurement pings for aggregate measurement. If you allow audience-measurement cookies, Analytics can use cookies for fuller measurement. On visits coming from a Mametas Reddit campaign, the same permission also allows the Reddit Pixel to record a PageVisit so Mametas can compare ad clicks with real landing-page visits.

Smart affiliate tools. If you allow them, Travelpayouts Drive can analyse site content and visitor behaviour and add or optimise affiliate elements where it identifies a travel-booking opportunity. It can change or add monetisation elements, but it does not choose which places Mametas includes, their editorial order, a Riviera Fit or Hotel Fit result, or the editorial verdict. Mametas does not load Drive before that choice. Travelpayouts may process technical, browser and interaction data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices are stored locally in your browser so the site can remember them. Because Travelpayouts Drive adds a new optional purpose, Mametas asks for a fresh choice even if you had previously accepted or rejected analytics.

You can change your decision at any time using “Cookie choices” in the footer. If you refuse or withdraw audience-measurement cookies, Analytics storage remains denied and Mametas attempts to remove Google Analytics cookies set on this domain. Cookieless consent-mode measurement may continue without Analytics cookies. Cookies already created by third parties may remain until they expire or are deleted through your browser controls.

Affiliate links

Some hotel and travel links are affiliate links: Booking.com links through the CJ Affiliate network, Expedia links, GetYourGuide links and, where enabled, links or affiliate elements handled through Travelpayouts Drive. If you follow one, the partner may recognise the referral and process data under its own privacy policy. Mametas may receive a commission if a qualifying booking follows, without increasing the price you pay.

Messages

Depending on the applicable law, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal data connected with an exchange, or object to its use. Contact hello@mametas.com. You may also contact your data-protection authority.
