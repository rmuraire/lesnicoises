PLAN · CAR OR NO CAR

Do you actually need a car on the French Riviera?

The useful answer is not yes or no. It depends on the base, the trip you want and on how many days the railway really stops helping.

NO CAR

The plan

Default to this

Nice base, 3-5 days, coastal towns, museums, accessible beaches, Monaco, Menton, Antibes and Cannes.

CAR FOR 1-2 DAYS

The smart compromise

Mostly coastal trip, plus Saint-Paul, Mougins, hinterland, isolated beaches or several villages in one day.

CAR ALL TRIP

Only when the trip demands it

Remote villa, hinterland-heavy itinerary, late hours off the main axes, rural multi-stop days or specific family logistics.

When the car is mostly baggage

In central Nice, Cannes or Antibes, and on a trip built around the TER, a car adds parking, traffic and the question of when to drive back. The coastal rail spine makes many days simpler without one.

When it becomes useful

Once you leave that rail spine, the answer changes: inland villages, certain beaches, panoramic roads, hiking trailheads and days combining places that are poorly linked to each other.

Three real cases, with numbers

CASE 1 · NICE OR MONACO

Parking for the day

Nice. On-street parking is capped at 2 hours 15 minutes: the first two hours are free once a day, ticket required, then a €25 penalty if you overstay. A full day in town ends in an underground car park, or in a tram park-and-ride, where parking is included with a €3.40 return or a €7 day pass.

Monaco. In public car parks the first hour is free, then about €7.30 for two hours and €24 for the day (late-2024 tariff, reviewed every year). By TER, Nice-Monaco costs €6.10 and takes about twenty minutes, with 65 return services a day.

Verdict: the train wins, no contest.

CASE 2 · SAINT-PAUL-DE-VENCE

The hill village

Without a car. TER to Cagnes-sur-Mer (€5, 15 minutes), then ZOU! bus 655 to Saint-Paul village or Fondation Maeght, 20 to 30 minutes. Roughly every 30 minutes on weekdays, every 45 minutes on Saturdays, every 40 to 50 minutes on Sundays.

By car. Metered car parks around the village: €2 an hour, €5.50 for three hours, free after 7pm. The Fondation Maeght car park is free.

Verdict: doable without a car for Saint-Paul alone. A car earns its keep if you chain Saint-Paul, Vence and other villages in one day.

CASE 3 · CAP-FERRAT IN SUMMER

The peninsula

Without a car. Bus 15 from Nice (Lycée Masséna stop), €1.70 a ride, to Saint-Jean harbour; the Passable-Rothschild stop serves Villa Ephrussi, until 11.45pm in July and August. Or TER to Beaulieu-sur-Mer, then the same bus.

By car. Parking is free from October to April, paid from May to the end of September. The Villa Ephrussi car park is free but small.

Verdict: in summer, take the bus. Out of season, the car becomes easy again.

THE MAMETAS CALL

Do not rent a car for five days because one day needs it. Build the trip without it first, then add 24 or 48 hours if a real part of the holiday earns the hassle.
