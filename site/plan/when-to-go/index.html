PLAN · WHEN TO GO

← Back to Plan

The right season depends on the trip you want.

The French Riviera does not have one universal “best time”. It has different trade-offs between sea, heat, price, light, crowds and seasonal operations.

Dates already fixed? Weather by season covers what it changes day to day.

NOV-MAR

The plan

City and culture first

Nice, Menton, Monaco, museums and restaurants. Shorter days; some beach operations become seasonal.

Very good if you are not chasing a beach week.
APR-JUN

Best balance

Longer days, easy day trips and less pressure than peak summer. Swimming is a different proposition in April and late June.

Our easiest window for an active first trip.
JUL-AUG

Sea, heat, pressure

Beaches and evenings at full volume; prices, traffic and booking pressure too. Start early, reserve what matters, cut the rest.

Excellent if summer is the trip, less so if you hate planning.
SEP-OCT

The Riviera exhales

Sea remains central early in the period, pace softens, good days are common. Later on, weather and seasonal operations need checking.

A very strong window, especially September.
MAMETAS

For a first 3-7 day trip where you actually want to move around, May-June and September remain the easiest windows to recommend, provided you book early: September is now as full as August. For the best balance of light, price and space, look at April and early October. July-August work very well if beach, heat and booking pressure are part of the product you want.

What the numbers say

September92% occupancy in Nice in 2025, same as August.
MayThe most expensive month in Monaco; Cannes at summer levels.
April and OctoberThe best balance of light, price and space.

September is no longer low season. In 2025 Nice hotels were 92% full in September, exactly as in August, at a barely lower average rate. The sea is still 22°C; the calm has gone.

June is Nice’s most expensive month; May is Monaco’s. In Cannes the Film Festival lifts May to summer levels: in 2025 an average night there cost over €690 in May as in July-August, against €194 in January.

April and October are the best value. Around 81% occupancy in Nice, average rates near €150, long days in April and a sea still swimmable in early October. November is the wettest month: 138 mm on average in Nice, more than January.

There is no truly empty month any more. In 2025 Riviera hotels set an occupancy record, and no month fell below 40%. Winter is quiet, not empty.
