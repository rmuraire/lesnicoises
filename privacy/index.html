Your data

Privacy & cookies

Mametas keeps optional tracking off until you choose. You can accept everything, reject everything or choose separately between audience measurement and smart affiliate tools. Refusing does not restrict the site.

Your optional choices

Audience measurement. If you allow it, Google Analytics helps us understand which pages are visited and how readers move through the site. Mametas does not load Google Analytics before that choice.

Smart affiliate tools. If you allow them, Travelpayouts Drive can analyse site content and visitor behaviour and add or optimise affiliate elements where it identifies a travel-booking opportunity. Mametas does not load Drive before that choice. Travelpayouts may process technical, browser and interaction data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices are stored locally in your browser so the site can remember them. Because Travelpayouts Drive adds a new optional purpose, Mametas asks for a fresh choice even if you had previously accepted or rejected analytics.

You can change your decision at any time using “Cookie choices” in the footer. When you withdraw a permission, Mametas stops loading that optional service on the next page load. It also attempts to remove Google Analytics cookies set on this domain; cookies already created by third parties may remain until they expire or are deleted through your browser controls.

Affiliate links

Some hotel and travel links are affiliate links, including Expedia links and, where enabled, links or affiliate elements handled through Travelpayouts Drive. If you follow one, the partner may recognise the referral and process data under its own privacy policy. Mametas may receive a commission if a qualifying booking follows, without increasing the price you pay.

Messages

If you email Mametas, the address and information you provide are used only to answer and manage that exchange. Do not send sensitive personal information that is not necessary for your request.

Your rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal data connected with an exchange, or object to its use. Contact hello@mametas.com. You may also contact your data-protection authority.

Updates

This page may change when the site adds a service or partner. Last updated: 10 September 2026.
