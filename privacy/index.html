Your data

Privacy & cookies

Mametas collects as little as possible. Analytics remains off until you choose to allow it, and refusing does not restrict the site.

Analytics choice

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics helps us understand which pages are visited and how readers move through the site. Mametas does not load Google Analytics before that choice. Your decision is stored locally in your browser so that the site remembers it.

You can change that decision at any time using “Cookie choices” in the footer. If you withdraw consent, Mametas stops future analytics collection and attempts to remove its analytics cookies from this domain.

Affiliate links

Some hotel links are affiliate links, currently including Expedia links. If you follow one, the partner may recognise the referral and process data under its own privacy policy. Mametas may receive a commission if a qualifying booking follows, without increasing the price you pay.

Messages

If you email Mametas, the address and information you provide are used only to answer and manage that exchange. Do not send sensitive personal information that is not necessary for your request.

Your rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal data connected with an exchange, or object to its use. Contact hello@mametas.com. You may also contact your data-protection authority.

Updates

This page may change when the site adds a service or partner. Last updated: 2 September 2026.
