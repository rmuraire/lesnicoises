BABYMOON · LOW FRICTION

The beautiful version of the Riviera is not always the easiest one.

For a babymoon, we would optimise for shade, lifts, short transfers, easy dinners and the ability to change your mind at 3pm. Glamour survives this surprisingly well.

FIRST, THE BORING IMPORTANT BIT

This is travel planning, not medical advice. Pregnancy and airline rules vary. Check your own situation with your doctor or midwife and your airline before booking. The NHS notes that many women prefer mid-pregnancy for travel and that airlines may apply restrictions or request documentation later in pregnancy.

Our base ranking is about friction, not beauty

01

Nice

Easiest overall. Airport tram, flat central areas, restaurants, pharmacies, taxis and trains for day trips. Choose the neighbourhood carefully and you can keep most days simple.

Antibes

Best for sand + slower pace. Old town, train station and beaches can work well together. The trade-off is a little more transfer time from the airport.

See Antibes → 03

Beaulieu / Cap-Ferrat

Best for resort comfort. Excellent if the hotel is doing a lot of the work. Villefranche itself is gorgeous but steep; gorgeous and low-friction are not synonyms.

The hotel checklist matters more than the star count

  • Lift: confirm it reaches your actual room floor. Old buildings enjoy exceptions.
  • Air conditioning: especially if you are considering late spring or summer.
  • Walk to dinner: a 12-minute picturesque climb can feel very different at the end of the day.
  • Breakfast / room service / easy food nearby: optional until suddenly not optional.
  • Pool or beach access: useful if the entire point is to stop doing things.
  • Taxi access: romantic pedestrian alleys are less romantic with luggage.

TRY HOTEL FIT

A four-day rhythm that leaves room to be tired

DAY 1

Arrive and stay local

If you are in Nice, take the tram or a taxi depending on luggage and energy. Check in. Sea. Dinner. Done.

DAY 2

One neighbourhood + one long lunch

Old Nice early, then stop. The goal is not to prove that pregnancy has failed to reduce your step count.

DAY 3

Choose the easiest beautiful excursion

Villefranche by train works if stairs and slopes feel fine that day. Antibes works if sand is the priority. A taxi to Cap-Ferrat works if the point is comfort, not transport purity.

DAY 4

Do less than you planned

Beach, hotel, spa treatment only if pregnancy-appropriate and cleared by the provider, early dinner. The Riviera remains there even if you do not complete it.

Places we would treat carefully

Èze Village: spectacular, but perched, stepped and not a low-effort default. The Exotic Garden sits at the top of the village and is not wheelchair accessible. Old Nice walk-ups: check the lift before booking. August afternoons: shade and air conditioning are logistics, not luxuries.

Mametas rule: if a day requires a train, a bus, a hill and a timed ticket, it is not a “relaxing day” just because the final photo contains the Mediterranean.
