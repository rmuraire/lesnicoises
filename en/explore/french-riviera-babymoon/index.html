The best babymoon is the one that does not require proving anything.

Choose comfort, shade, easy transport and a hotel you actually want to spend time in. The Riviera will still be there if you skip the 400-step viewpoint.

MAMETAS SAYS

Pick the easiest version of the Riviera, not the most ambitious one. Nice is usually the strongest base because it gives you sea, restaurants, an airport tram, dense everyday services and coastal trains without needing a car.

Best bases

BEST ALL-ROUNDER

Nice

Flat-ish central areas, tram from the airport, taxis everywhere, many restaurants and easy coastal trains. Choose Carré d’Or, Musiciens or a central seafront hotel if reducing walking friction matters.

Avoid unnecessary friction: check lifts, stairs and uphill access before booking Old Nice.

QUIETEST SEA RHYTHM

Beaulieu / Villefranche

Better for a slower beach-first trip. Beaulieu is particularly useful if you want a flatter small-town rhythm and a train station.

The catch: fewer choices at night and less redundancy if you need something spontaneously.

Build days around energy, not ambition

  • One main outing per day is enough.
  • Keep the hotel within easy reach at midday.
  • In hot months, front-load walking before lunch.
  • Use trains for Antibes, Cannes, Monaco and Menton instead of sitting in traffic.
  • Plan at least one day with no destination at all.

Easy outings

Villefranche: short train, waterfront, lunch, back. Antibes: old town + Picasso Museum + beach. Monaco: train plus lifts/escalators, but choose station exits carefully. Cap-Ferrat: beautiful, but taxi can be the rational option if walking feels like work.

Hotel Fit matters more here

A lift, good air conditioning, a proper breakfast, quiet room and easy taxi access can matter more than a famous rooftop. This is exactly the kind of trip where a “beautiful” hotel can still be the wrong hotel.

TRY HOTEL FIT

Summer: the catch nobody should romanticise

July and August can be hot, bright and crowded. If your dates are flexible, late May, June or September usually make the same coastline easier to live with.
