ABOUT

An independent guide, born from a Niçois point of view.

We love this region enough to want to share it. And enough not to pretend that everything here is wonderful.

Where Mametas comes from

Mametas was created by someone from Nice with a very simple idea: recreate the conversation you have when somebody comes for the first time and asks, “what would you really recommend?” Not the complete list. Not twenty places that might work. The choice you would make for somebody you actually want to see come back.

The French Riviera is spectacular, occasionally excessive, often badly explained and much more varied than its postcard image. You can love Nice, the sea, the villages, the hotels and the grand institutions without pretending everything is equally worth your time. Mametas comes from that kind of affection: knowing a place well enough to know what you can skip too.

WHY THE MAMETAS?

Because a grandmother usually knows how to make a call.

In Nice, grandmothers belong to the emotional landscape almost as much as the blue chairs, the façades and the sea. To us they represent memory, taste, frankness and that very local ability to tell you “go there” or “do not waste your time” without turning it into a presentation. We gave that voice to the guide because it is exactly what Mametas is trying to do.

The five Mametas you see on the site are editorial characters. They are not presented as real people. The places, research, sources, checks and editorial decisions are real.

What we actually do

Hotels, restaurants, beaches, museums and villages are selected by cross-checking official sources, practical information, maps, menus, programmes, reviews and public feedback. Then we make a call. If somewhere has not been personally visited, we do not pretend otherwise.

Does that make us objective? No. That would be a fairly silly promise. Every recommendation is a filter. Ours simply tries to have less diplomacy to protect, fewer people to charm and more freedom to say that a magnificent view does not necessarily rescue a mediocre lunch.

HOUSE RULE

The recommendation comes first. Everything else comes after.

And the money?

Some links, particularly hotel links, may be affiliate links. If you book through them, Mametas may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That helps pay for the site. It does not decide the selection. An affiliate link does not buy an opinion.

Our incentive is fairly simple: if we keep sending you somewhere mediocre to make a commission, you will stop coming back. We would rather you came back.

Talk to us

Mametas is an independent editorial guide. Tourist offices, hotels, restaurants, beaches, cultural venues, photographers and journalists can contact us directly. We would rather receive one useful fact or one strong image than a six-page press release.
