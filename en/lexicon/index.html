SPEAK A LITTLE NIÇOIS

The mini-lexicon

We keep using the words, pichoun. Fair enough. Here is what they mean.

Mèfi

Watch out. Be careful. The one to learn first.

Pichoun

Little one, kid. Usually affectionate. Context still applies.

Cagade

An avoidable cock-up. Precisely the kind of thing Mametas is trying to save you from.

Dégun

Nobody. “Y a dégun” means there is nobody there. A fairly ambitious request on the Riviera in August.

Ficanas

Nosy gossips. A local skill which, used properly, can produce excellent recommendations.

Empégué

Someone looking distinctly out of it. Including behind the wheel, unfortunately.

Niocou

A fool, a simpleton. Try not to give anyone a reason to use it.

Paillassou

Someone not exactly displaying peak judgement.

Hu

A local interjection. Put it roughly where the emotion requires; comprehension tends to arrive through exposure.

THE RULE

Do not force the accent. Dégun asked for that.

Independent editorial selection. Local vocabulary used with affection and very little linguistic discipline.
