DAY TRIPS

Walking Cap-Ferrat: three coastal paths

Cap-Ferrat is very good at making it look as though everyone owns a villa behind the pines. Good news: the coastal path belongs to the rest of us.

Maurice Rouvier

About 1.3 km and easy, between Beaulieu-sur-Mer and Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat. Sea views without turning the day into an expedition.

Pointe Saint-Hospice

Around 1.8 km, a wilder-feeling loop around the point, with coves and pines.

Tour du Cap-Ferrat

Around 4.8 km and roughly two hours according to the tourism office. Bring water, proper shoes and check access after heavy swell or strong wind.

MAMETAS SAYS

A good day trip leaves empty time. If you need to check your watch every twenty minutes, remove a stop.
