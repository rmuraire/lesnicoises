ART & CULTURE • VILLEFRANCHE-SUR-MER

Saint-Pierre Chapel

Cocteau inside a fishermen’s chapel a few steps from the harbour. Short visit, strong personality.

MAMETAS SAYS

Exactly the kind of detour Mametas likes: small, singular and impossible to separate completely from the place around it.

Why we go

Because Cocteau turned a chapel tied to Villefranche’s fishermen into something unmistakably personal. It does not need half a day; the concentration is part of the charm.

What to actually look at

The wall decorations, their relationship with the sea and the way the small interior changes once you remember the harbour is still only a few metres away.

PRACTICAL

On the harbour in Villefranche-sur-Mer. Easy to combine with the old town, waterfront and a swim at Les Marinières. Check the day’s opening hours before setting out.

Do not cross the whole Riviera for a fifteen-minute visit. But if you are already in Villefranche, walking past without going in feels unnecessarily stubborn.

Independent editorial selection. Opening hours change. Opinions do too.
