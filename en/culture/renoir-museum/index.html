ART & CULTURE • CAGNES-SUR-MER

Renoir Museum

A house, a studio and olive trees: the kind of museum where the place matters almost as much as the works.

MAMETAS SAYS

Les Collettes is really about Renoir’s final years in the landscape he chose. More intimate than a major museum, and better when paired with Haut-de-Cagnes.

Why we go

To see the artist in context rather than as a row of pictures detached from everything around them. The house, studio and estate make the visit feel human in scale.

What to actually look at

The studio, the rooms and especially the relationship between the house, garden and old olive trees. Here, stepping outside is part of the visit.

PRACTICAL

Cagnes-sur-Mer. Allow half a day if you combine it with Haut-de-Cagnes. Check the day’s opening hours before setting out.

On a very short first trip, it is not an absolute must. If Renoir or artists’ homes matter to you, the ranking changes quickly.

Independent editorial selection. Opening hours change. Opinions do too.
