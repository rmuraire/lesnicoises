ART & CULTURE

Culture

The Riviera is not only somewhere to tan, pichoun. Nobody is forcing you into a museum, but do not come back after three beach clubs claiming there is “nothing to see”.

The Riviera, where the walls occasionally have something to say

Foundations, villas, museums and chapels worth putting a shirt back on for. A selective edit from Nice to Menton, Antibes, Cannes, Vence, Cagnes and Monaco. Not a directory. A choice.

6 museums worth a proper look
