ART & CULTURE

Culture

The Riviera is not only somewhere to tan, pichoun. Nobody is forcing you into a museum, but do not come back after three beach clubs claiming there is “nothing to see”.

The Riviera, where the walls occasionally have something to say

Foundations, villas, museums and a few places worth putting a shirt back on for. We start with ten addresses across Nice, Menton, Antibes, Cannes and Monaco. Not a directory. A selection.

Nice

6 museums worth a proper look

Matisse, Chagall, Lascaris, photography, Masséna and Fine Arts. The sea can wait two hours.
