Palais Lascaris

A Baroque palace in Old Nice, with historic instruments and interiors doing most of the work before you read a single label.

MAMETAS SAYS

Probably the easiest cultural stop to slip into an Old Nice day. You go in for the palace and stay for the details.

Why we go

Because the building avoids the feeling of “adding a museum to the schedule”. Monumental staircase, salons, painted ceilings and musical instruments give even impatient visitors something immediate to look at.

What to actually look at

The architecture first. Look up before reading anything. The historic instruments then provide a second reason not to walk straight back out after ten minutes.

PRACTICAL

In the heart of Old Nice. Easy to combine with the market, the lanes and lunch nearby. Check the day’s opening hours before setting out.

If you have one cultural hour in the middle of a central Nice day, this is a very strong candidate.

Independent editorial selection. Opening hours change. Opinions do too.
