Palais Lascaris

You can go in for the instruments. You may stay because the Baroque palace is perfectly capable of stealing the show.

MAMETAS SAYS

The easiest museum to fold into a wander through Old Nice: central, compact and distinctive enough not to feel like cultural homework.

Why go

This 17th-century palace is one of Nice’s strongest Baroque interiors. It also houses a major collection of historic musical instruments, giving it another reason to exist beyond the painted ceilings.

What to look at

The monumental staircase, decorated salons and frescoes, then the instruments. Do not try to read everything: the place works first through atmosphere and accumulation.

PRACTICAL

15 rue Droite, Old Nice. Closed Tuesdays. Allow around an hour, longer if historic instruments are your thing.

Very easy to fit between the market, lunch and the old town. Which is precisely why there is almost no good excuse to miss it.
