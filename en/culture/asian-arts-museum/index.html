Asian Arts Museum

A clean change of pace near Arenas: Asian collections and architecture in a completely different register.

MAMETAS SAYS

Not the museum we would put first on a three-day debut. Which is exactly why it becomes useful when you want something beyond the usual Matisse-Chagall-Old Nice trio.

Why we go

For a complete change of register: Asian art collections inside a contemporary building that feels very different from the Riviera’s heritage-heavy museums.

What to actually look at

Works from India, China, Japan and Southeast Asia, but also the graphic relationship between the building, the water and its clean volumes.

PRACTICAL

West Nice, near Arenas. Especially logical if your day already brings you to this side of the city. Check the day’s opening hours before setting out.

Not a universal “must”. A very good choice for travellers who would rather pick the museum that suits them than repeat the same list.

Independent editorial selection. Opening hours change. Opinions do too.
