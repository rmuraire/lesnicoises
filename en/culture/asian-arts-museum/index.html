Asian Arts Museum

A free Kenzo Tange museum set on water near the airport. Nice occasionally has very efficient ways to fill an awkward final hour.

MAMETAS SAYS

Not a first-weekend essential. A very good choice, though, if you like architecture, have already seen the classics or have an inconvenient gap before a flight.

Why go

The museum presents Asian art collections inside a building designed by Kenzo Tange. The dialogue between square, circle, white marble and water gives the place a very distinct presence.

What to look at

Start with the building, then move through the collections by cultural region. Its position beside Parc Phoenix and the airport makes it unusually practical as well.

PRACTICAL

405 Promenade des Anglais, Nice, by Parc Phoenix. Closed Tuesdays. Permanent collections are free; check seasonal opening hours.

Ideal before or after the airport, or as a cultural plan B. On your very first day in Nice, start elsewhere.
