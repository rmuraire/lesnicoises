ART & CULTURE • VENCE

Rosary Chapel

Matisse strips almost everything back to light, line and a few colours. Small place, strong reason to go to Vence.

MAMETAS SAYS

If the Matisse Museum explains a body of work, the chapel shows what happens when Matisse designs an entire place. Brief, deliberate and impossible to mistake for one more church.

Why we go

To see drawing, stained glass, ceramics, light and liturgical objects work as one composition. The project is compact, which is exactly the point: almost nothing is accidental.

What to actually look at

The coloured light, the black lines on the ceramics and the economy of the whole room. Do not look for an accumulation of works; the work is the ensemble.

PRACTICAL

In Vence. Fit it into a half-day or full day inland. Check the day’s opening hours before heading up.

If Matisse, design or architecture matters to you, make the detour. If you only collect “major museum” checkmarks, leave it to someone else.
