Villa Arson

An art school, an exhibition centre and architecture that gives you as much reason to look outside as inside.

Mametas CheckedPractical information rechecked 23 September 2026 · official sources
MAMETAS SAYS

A good way to move beyond the Matisse-Chagall-Old-Nice trio without adding a museum just to fill the schedule. Villa Arson has real character and the art centre is free.

Why we go

Villa Arson combines a national art school with a contemporary art centre. The programme changes, but the architecture, terraces and gardens already make it a destination in its own right.

What to actually look at

The temporary exhibitions, but also the building itself: concrete, stone, patios and views over Nice. Here the container deserves almost as much attention as the content.
