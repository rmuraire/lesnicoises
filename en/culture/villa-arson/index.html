Villa Arson

Contemporary art, concrete, gardens and terraces. For once, Nice is not asking you to look only in the rear-view mirror.

MAMETAS SAYS

Villa Arson is the useful counterpoint to Baroque palaces and Belle Époque villas: a place where both architecture and exhibitions speak in the present tense.

Why we go

For contemporary art, obviously, but also for an architectural complex worth seeing in its own right. The art school and exhibition centre give it a different energy from a heritage museum.

What to actually look at

The current exhibitions, then the circulation spaces, terraces, gardens and the way the building settles into the hillside. Do not sprint for the exit after the last gallery.

PRACTICAL

North Nice. Pick it over one more heritage museum if you want a contemporary register. Check the current programme and opening hours before setting out.

Not an automatic first-trip must. If architecture or contemporary art is your thing, it moves very high up the list.
