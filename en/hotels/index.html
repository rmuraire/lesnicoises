Selected hotels

Good addresses, for different reasons.

We are not building a census. We are building enough choice to make a decision without spending the evening comparing 143 beige rooms.

Hotel 66 Nice

For train-heavy trips. Station-friendly, practical and refreshingly uninterested in pretending logistics do not matter.

Hôtel Apollinaire

For the all-round first trip. Central enough for evenings, sensible enough for day trips.

Hôtel La Pérouse

For romantic Nice. Sea, Old Nice and a location that earns its premium; less clever if the station runs your week.

Hôtel Villa Victoria

For central calm. A garden-led answer when you want Nice nearby rather than in the bedroom.

La Villa Port d’Antibes

For Antibes on foot. Old-town geography with enough polish to make staying in feel plausible.

Hôtel La Place

For a sensible Antibes budget. Central without requiring the hotel to become the holiday.

Welcome Hotel

For the harbour fantasy. All 35 rooms face the bay; the compromise is choosing Villefranche’s beauty over Nice’s transport machine.

Hôtel Le Comte de Nice

For quieter value. Beaulieu without automatically entering palace economics.

Hôtel de Londres

For character at mid-range. Menton is already softer than Monaco; there is no need to make the hotel shout.

Hôtel Napoléon Menton

For sea + pool. A four-star eastern base when slowing down is part of the plan, not an administrative failure.

Villa Genesis

For boutique luxury. A five-star villa near the sea, better suited to quiet indulgence than nightly Monaco theatre.

Le Cavendish

For Cannes without palace scale. A boutique counterpoint when the Croisette’s full theatre is not the point of the trip.
