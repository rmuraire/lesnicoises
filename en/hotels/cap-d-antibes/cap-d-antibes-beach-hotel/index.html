HOTEL • CAP D'ANTIBES

Cap d'Antibes Beach Hotel

If you came to the Cap for the sea, you may as well actually sleep beside it.

Cap d'Antibes Beach Hotel is a 35-room-and-suite five-star hotel right on the water, with a private beach, infinity pool and two restaurants including Michelin-starred Les Pêcheurs.

Architect Bernard Dubois reworked the hotel in a language mixing Palm Springs and the Mediterranean. Much more design than old-school Riviera, although the pines and sea wisely do the rest of the work.

AreaWaterfront
Format35 rooms and suites

THE LES NIÇOISES VERDICT

A genuine waterfront hotel, not a place selling you 'beach' because you can glimpse it between two apartment blocks.

Why we keep it

Here, beach and pool are not accessories. They genuinely structure the stay, which makes the price easier to justify if your plan is to move as little as possible.

The 35-room scale also keeps a degree of intimacy that large waterfront resorts quickly lose.

Who it suits

  • You want to spend a lot of time at the beach and pool.
  • You like contemporary design.
  • You want Cap d'Antibes more than Cannes nightlife.

Mèfi if...

  • You plan to do Nice and Monaco every day.
  • You want an economical stay.
  • You need a city immediately outside the hotel.
