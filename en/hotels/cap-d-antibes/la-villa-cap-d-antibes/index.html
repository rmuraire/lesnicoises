HOTEL • CAP D'ANTIBES

Hôtel La Villa Cap d'Antibes

Cap d'Antibes without having to refinance a small principality.

La Villa is a 26-room four-star hotel in a quiet part of Cap d'Antibes. According to the hotel, every room has a balcony or terrace, and the property is organised around a garden and pool.

It is a much simpler proposition than the grand waterfront names, which is exactly the appeal. You pay for the Cap, quiet and pool, not for a ballroom you will cross once.

THE MAMETAS VERDICT

A human-scale address that lets you stay on the Cap without turning the trip into a palace competition.

Why we keep it

With only 26 rooms, the atmosphere is closer to a large house than a resort.

The pool and garden make the quiet hours of the day genuinely useful, especially in summer once you have accepted that between 1pm and 4pm the sun has nothing left to prove.

Who it suits

  • You want a smaller, quieter hotel.
  • A pool matters but a private beach is not essential.
  • You want a somewhat saner price point on the Cap.

Mèfi if...

  • You want to be directly on the water.
  • You want central Cannes energy.
  • You require a major spa and ten restaurants on site.
