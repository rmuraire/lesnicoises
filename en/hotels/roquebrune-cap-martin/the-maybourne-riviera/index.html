HOTEL • ROQUEBRUNE-CAP-MARTIN

The Maybourne Riviera

An architectural object that seems to have forgotten a cliff is not normally furniture.

Clinging to the cliffs of Roquebrune-Cap-Martin above Monaco, The Maybourne Riviera is an uncompromising contemporary luxury hotel with very open Mediterranean views.

Stays include access to Surrenne Riviera wellness facilities and, in season according to the hotel, a private beach plus shuttle service to Monaco and the beach. It is designed to remove most logistical reasons to leave.

AreaCliffs above Monaco
FormatContemporary luxury hotel
Best forArchitecture, views, spa
BudgetExtreme
THE MAMETAS VERDICT

Spectacular, very expensive and distinctive enough that your money does not appear to have purchased beige marble alone.

Why we keep it

The architecture and view are the main product here. If neither moves you, there are considerably cheaper ways to sleep near Monaco.

The shuttle to Monaco and the beach also addresses the main inconvenience of a hotel suspended on a cliff: the cliff.

Who it suits

  • You want the hotel itself to be a destination.
  • Architecture, spa and views matter greatly.
  • Monaco should be close without actually sleeping in Monaco.

Mèfi if...

  • You like stepping outside and improvising on foot.
  • Budget is a rational decision criterion.
  • You prefer old stone to highly contemporary lines.
