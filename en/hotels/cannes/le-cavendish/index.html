HOTEL • CANNES

Le Cavendish

Cannes without a marble lobby the size of a prefecture. It exists.

Le Cavendish is a 34-room four-star boutique hotel in a Belle Époque building on Boulevard Carnot. The Croisette and Palais des Festivals are listed around five minutes away on foot.

It keeps its period lift and parquet floors and feels far more domestic than the seafront palaces. Guests are also offered an evening aperitif. Finally, a public policy we can support.

AreaBoulevard Carnot
Format34 rooms, 4-star

BudgetHigh
THE MAMETAS VERDICT

A more human-scale option if you want Cannes on foot without financing an army of valets every evening.

Why we keep it

The smaller scale changes the experience. This feels like a city hotel rather than a festival infrastructure with bedrooms attached.

It is particularly interesting if you want Cannes but would rather spend the difference at dinner than on another twenty square metres of lobby.

Who it suits

  • You prefer smaller hotels to palaces.
  • You want to walk to the Palais and Croisette.
  • You like buildings with some character.

Mèfi if...

  • You need a pool or private beach inside the hotel.
  • Your Cannes fantasy requires a sea-facing room.
  • You like the hotel to be more famous than you are.
