HOTEL • CANNES

Hôtel Martinez

If you are going to do Cannes, sometimes you may as well stop pretending you came for discretion.

Martinez is one of the Croisette's big names, with 410 rooms and suites in a contemporary Art Deco language inspired by the 1930s.

It is a very large hotel in the literal sense. It works especially well for people who want Cannes immediately: Croisette, beach, grand scale, palace service and considerably less time spent explaining your address to a taxi driver.

THE LES NIÇOISES VERDICT

A palace that looks exactly like many people's idea of Cannes. Sometimes the cliché works because this is where it was invented.

Why we keep it

The Croisette location makes festivals, congresses and short stays dramatically easier. You are already where much of Cannes happens.

Sea-view rooms with balconies make obvious sense here. If you pay Martinez prices but strip out the very thing that makes the Croisette appealing to save a little, compare the categories carefully before clicking.

Who it suits

  • You are coming for the Festival, a congress or a classic first Cannes experience.
  • You like large hotels with extensive services.
  • The beach and Croisette are part of the trip.

Mèfi if...

  • You are after a confidential hideaway.
  • You dislike the energy of very large hotels.
  • Your budget is already looking suspiciously at Cannes.
