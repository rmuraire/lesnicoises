HOTEL • CANNES

Hôtel Barrière Le Majestic Cannes

For people who want Cannes exactly as Cannes likes to present itself.

Facing the Palais des Festivals, Le Majestic is the most logistically obvious address in this selection for an event at the Palais. The five-star hotel has a private beach, heated outdoor pool and spa.

The style and services are classic grand-hotel territory. If your stay revolves around a congress, festival or Croisette meetings, the location can provide a very concrete luxury: walking.

AreaCroisette / Palais des Festivals

THE LES NIÇOISES VERDICT

Not the place to role-play as a mysterious local. The place to avoid losing forty minutes in a taxi when your conference badge starts at 8:30am.

Why we keep it

Proximity to the Palais has real value during major events. On certain weeks, a few hundred metres are worth more than a great many spa promises.

The beach and pool also stop Cannes becoming nothing but badges, lobbies and meetings.

Who it suits

  • You are coming for an event at the Palais.
  • You want Croisette and beach in the same place.
  • You appreciate classic, service-heavy grand hotels.

Mèfi if...

  • You want a residential atmosphere.
  • You want to escape every bit of central Cannes energy.
  • Your primary metric is euros per square metre.
