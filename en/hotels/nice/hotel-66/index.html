HOTEL • NICE

Hotel 66 Nice

A practical base near the station, without pretending that logistics are beneath the Riviera.

Hotel 66 is the sort of Nice address that makes sense when the city is your launch pad rather than the whole performance. The appeal is straightforward: central geography, easy access to Nice-Ville and a room that does not require a sea-view surcharge to justify itself.

For a first trip without a car, that usefulness matters. Early train to Menton, late return from Antibes, airport transfer, dinner in the centre: fewer moving parts, more holiday.

THE MAMETAS VERDICT

The station-friendly answer the Nice shortlist was missing. The station area is useful before it is romantic.

Why we keep it

It widens the catalogue beyond luxury and solves a real first-trip problem: how to stay central without paying for a hotel experience you barely use.

Who it suits

  • You prefer location and trip logic to unnecessary hotel theatre.
  • You plan to use Nice as a base for the coast.
