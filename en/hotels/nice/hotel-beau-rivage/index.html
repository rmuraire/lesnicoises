Hotel Beau Rivage

Old Nice on one side, the sea on the other. Geography doing almost all the selling.

Beau Rivage is a 114-room-and-suite four-star at the entrance to Old Nice, between Cours Saleya and the Promenade des Anglais. The hotel describes a contemporary boutique style; the tourist office also lists it as Nice Rainbow and Green Key.

This is a location-first choice. You can walk to dinner, the market and the sea, and the hotel also has access to the Beau Rivage beach operation nearby.

AreaOld Nice / seafront
Format114 rooms & suites
LabelNice Rainbow
BudgetUpper-mid
THE MAMETAS VERDICT

For a first Nice trip, this is one of those addresses where the map is almost the amenity.

Who it suits

  • You want Old Nice and the seafront in the same stay.
  • You plan to walk far more than you plan to use taxis.
  • You want a mainstream four-star that also carries Nice Rainbow.

Mèfi if...

  • You want a quiet residential neighbourhood.
  • You are choosing Nice mainly for train day trips.
  • You prefer a tiny, intimate hotel.
