Hôtel Villa Victoria

Central Nice with a softer rhythm: a good answer when calm matters but exile does not.

Villa Victoria gives the Nice shortlist something it badly needed: a central address whose appeal is not built around being on the Promenade. The garden and residential feel make it better suited to travellers who want to come back to something quieter after the day.

You remain close enough to the centre to walk most evenings, while avoiding the logic that every good Nice hotel must face the sea and charge accordingly.
