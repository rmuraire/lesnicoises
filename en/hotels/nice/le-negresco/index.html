HOTEL • NICE

Le Negresco

You do not book Le Negresco because you like beige. Thank God.

On the Promenade des Anglais, Le Negresco makes no attempt to be discreet. Pink dome, historic salons, art everywhere and rooms that jump between periods and styles: this is a hotel that has become part of Nice's scenery.

The hotel lists 102 rooms and 28 suites, mixing period furniture and artworks with contemporary design. If you wanted a completely interchangeable grand hotel, pichoun, you have chosen rather badly.

THE MAMETAS VERDICT

An icon with enough personality to justify doing more than staring at it from a taxi. The rate is fully aware that it sleeps inside a legend.

Why we keep it

Its main virtue is exactly what many hotels work hard to remove: eccentricity. The rooms do not all come from the same catalogue and the building operates almost as much as an art collection as a palace.

The Promenade is outside the door. For a first stay in Nice, the geography is hard to beat if sea and centre matter more than absolute silence.

Who it suits

  • You want a hotel that actually has something to say.
  • You like art, historic interiors and hotels with almost excessive personality.
  • You want the Promenade without exiling yourself from central Nice.

Mèfi if...

  • You came for Japanese minimalism. Very much not the point.
  • Your budget needs a calm, rational conversation.
  • You want a discreet address whose façade nobody recognises.
