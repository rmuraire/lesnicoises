HOTELS • NICE

Where to stay in Nice : neighbourhoods that work

The right hotel matters. The right neighbourhood matters more. You rarely sleep in the lobby.

In Nice, the neighbourhood often matters more than the hotel. A perfect room forty minutes from everything you want to do remains a perfect room you will spend less time in.

Old NiceMaximum charm, variable silence.
Port / RiquierVery practical for the eastern coast and regional trains.
Carré d’OrCentral, near the sea, priced accordingly.
FOR A FIRST TRIP

Stay somewhere between Jean Médecin, Carré d’Or, Old Nice and the Port. If a tram or train station is within fifteen minutes on foot, you have already solved much of the logistics.

Old Nice : for walking out the door

The most atmospheric choice if you want Cours Saleya and dinner on foot. The compromise is obvious : Old Nice is alive. So are its windows.

1 RUE HONORÉ UGO

Hôtel du Couvent

The high-end choice that genuinely has a reason to exist here.

A restored 17th-century convent with 88 rooms, gardens and Roman-inspired baths. Spectacular, extremely considered and right inside Old Nice. Not the budget option. Nobody was really expecting that.

Rauba Capeu : for the view

11 QUAI RAUBA CAPEU

Hôtel La Pérouse

For waking up above the Baie des Anges without sitting in the middle of the Promenade.

53 rooms and suites, four stars, a pool carved into the rock and a position between Old Nice, Castle Hill and the sea.

Carré d’Or / Fleurs : for the compromise

Close to the sea, centre and station without sleeping inside Old Nice. A strong first-trip location.

3 AVENUE DES FLEURS

Hôtel Amour Nice

For a younger, less palace-like mood.

A small design-led hotel with a seasonal rooftop and affiliated beach on the Promenade des Anglais. The atmosphere is part of the point.

10 RUE DE RIVOLI

Villa Rivoli

For something more classic and contained.

24 individually decorated rooms in an 1890 Belle Époque house, a three-star hotel very close to the Promenade.

Port / Riquier : for getting around

21 BOULEVARD DE RIQUIER

Mama Shelter Nice

For a contemporary option near the Port.

102 rooms, rooftop and a seasonal pool reserved for hotel guests. The neighbourhood feels more everyday than Carré d’Or. That is part of its appeal.

Do not pay for a sea view if you plan to spend all day outside and close the curtains at night. The Mediterranean does not issue refunds.
