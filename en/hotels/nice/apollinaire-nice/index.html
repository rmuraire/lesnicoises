Hôtel Apollinaire Nice

A very useful hotel. Coming from us, that is a compliment.

On Boulevard Dubouchage, Apollinaire is a 95-room-and-suite four-star hotel in a nineteenth-century building renovated in 2021. It sits between Jean Médecin, the green corridor and Old Nice.

No grand palace performance here. The appeal is more useful: central location, a patio, easy tram access and enough comfort to spend the rest of your budget on something other than a rooftop pool you may never use.

AreaDubouchage / centre
Format95 rooms and suites
Best forCentral, practical, 4-star
BudgetUpper-mid to high
THE MAMETAS VERDICT

The rational choice in this selection, which does not mean boring. Very well placed, without requiring every breakfast to finance a dome.

Why we keep it

Durandy tram stop is listed around 200 metres away with direct airport access. Jean Médecin and the main station are also easy to reach.

This is the sort of hotel that works well when Nice is your base and you actually plan to leave the hotel rather than stage a spiritual retreat in the lobby.

Who it suits

  • You prioritise location and comfort over spectacle.
  • You are coming without a car.
  • You would like to keep some of the budget for restaurants and day trips.

Mèfi if...

  • A pool is essential.
  • You require a sea view from bed.
  • The hotel itself needs to be the main event of the trip.
