HOTEL • MENTON

Hôtel de Londres

A Menton address with enough personality to make staying east feel like a choice, not an overflow plan.

Hôtel de Londres gives Menton a more characterful mid-range option in our shortlist. It suits travellers who want the softer end of the Riviera without converting the stay into a luxury-hotel exercise.

The city is walkable, the coast is close and the station keeps Monaco and Nice connected. What you give up is centrality to the whole Riviera: Menton is a destination first, a universal base second.
