HOTEL • MONACO

Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo

Subtlety left somewhere between the marble staircase and Casino Square. Fine.

On Casino Square, Hôtel de Paris is one of those addresses that summarises Monaco before you have even reached the room. Monte-Carlo SBM's 2025-2026 documentation lists 206 rooms and suites.

Guests have access to the seasonal Wellness Sky Club and direct access to Thermes Marins Monte-Carlo, with indoor pool and wellness facilities. This is full Monaco, with no attempt to suggest you came for simplicity.

THE LES NIÇOISES VERDICT

If you want Monaco, it is difficult to get more Monaco than this. If you do not want Monaco, why exactly are you reading this page?

Why we keep it

The Casino Square location removes much of the transport friction from a Monte-Carlo-centred stay.

The spa, seasonal rooftop and restaurants also turn the hotel into a destination in its own right. At this price level, that is probably for the best.

Who it suits

  • You want an iconic Monaco experience.
  • Budget is not the first filter.
  • Spa, gastronomy and palace service are central to the stay.

Mèfi if...

  • You want the simple, bohemian Riviera.
  • You would like to avoid all formality.
  • You perform mental arithmetic with every cocktail.
