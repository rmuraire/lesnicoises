Decide before booking

Around Saint-Tropez, a few kilometres change a lot. The classic mistake is booking “near Saint-Tropez” and discovering that “near” has a very optimistic summer definition.

The shortlist is deliberately short. The point is not to recreate Booking with fewer filters, but to reduce the choice to a few understandable ways of travelling.
