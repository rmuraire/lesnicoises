HOTEL • ANTIBES

La Villa Port d’Antibes & Spa

A polished Antibes base for people who want the old town and port without moving out to the Cap.

La Villa Port d’Antibes fills a useful gap between a simple town hotel and the more secluded Cap d’Antibes experience. It keeps you close to the old town, the harbour and the station while still feeling like a deliberate hotel choice.

That combination makes sense for a two- to five-night western Riviera base: beach and restaurants nearby, Cannes easy by train, and no need to turn every outing into a taxi.
