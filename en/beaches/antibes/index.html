BEACHES

Antibes beaches: 5 places for sand, old walls and the Cap

Antibes has something Nice mostly does not: sand. Do not get too excited; everyone else has heard about it.

Vieil Antibes

La Gravette

Sand, protected by the old walls and easy from the port. A practical family option and a swim that does not require an expedition.

Sortie du Vieil Antibes vers le Cap

Ponteil

A long sandy beach with shallow water and summer facilities. More practical than secret, which is sometimes exactly what you need.

Au pied du Cap d’Antibes

La Salis

Sand and shallow water at the foot of Cap d’Antibes, near the Calvaire path to the Garoupe. Swimming plus a walk works very well here.

Cap d’Antibes

La Garoupe

Small beaches and coves near the start of the Tirepoil coastal path. Prettier, tighter and with some unsupervised areas.

Côté ouest

Juan-les-Pins

A long stretch of sandy public and private beaches with plenty of services. Not wild. Easy.

MÈFI

On the Riviera, a “secret” beach with a car park, a map pin and three thousand reviews is not very secret. Choose the time of day instead.
