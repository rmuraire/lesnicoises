BEACHES • NICE

Where to swim in Nice

Nice does not have a secret beach. If someone promises one three minutes from the Promenade, they probably also know an 'authentic' restaurant nearby.

The coastline is easy to understand. Along the Promenade des Anglais, long pebble beaches are central and convenient. East of the Port, smaller rocky swimming spots tend to feel more local.

BOULEVARD FRANCK PILATTE

La Réserve

East of the Port. Smaller, with a more local feel and less directly connected to the Promenade crowds. It is not empty. Nothing beautiful in Nice stays empty for long.

AVENUE JEAN LORRAIN

Coco Beach

Rocks rather than a classic beach. Come to swim and enjoy the setting, not to build a small private estate around your towel.

QUAI DES ÉTATS-UNIS

Ponchettes

Extremely central, just below Old Nice. Excellent if you want sea and city with no logistics. Less ideal if happiness requires ten metres between you and the next towel.

Our rule

In July and August, come early. The advice is unoriginal because it works.
