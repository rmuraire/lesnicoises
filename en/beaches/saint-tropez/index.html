BEACHES

Saint-Tropez beaches: 5 options before saying “Pampelonne”

Pampelonne is beautiful. It is also in Ramatuelle and the whole world knows its name. Saint-Tropez has other places to put a towel.

Entrée de Saint-Tropez

La Bouillabaisse

A wide sandy beach facing the Massif des Maures, divided between public shore and private beach venues. Excellent at sunset.

Vieille ville

La Ponche / Fontanette

A tiny beach at the foot of old Saint-Tropez. You come as much for the setting as for the swim.

Sous la Citadelle

Les Graniers

A small sandy cove below the Citadel, beyond the marine cemetery, with clear water and a more natural feel. Small also means: arrive early.

Baie des Canoubiers

Les Canoubiers

Sand and umbrella pines in a more residential setting. Reachable on foot or by boat depending on where you start.

À l’est du village

Les Salins

A family beach with clear water; dried posidonia can wash ashore depending on currents. It is not “dirty”: it is part of the ecosystem.

MÈFI

On the Riviera, a “secret” beach with a car park, a map pin and three thousand reviews is not very secret. Choose the time of day instead.
