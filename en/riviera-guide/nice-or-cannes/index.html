RIVIERA GUIDE • COMPARISON

Nice or Cannes: where should you stay?

Nice or Cannes? Mèfi, pichoun: if your main criterion is “which one looks better with my linen shirt on Instagram?”, we can probably do better than that.

Choose Nice if...

You want a real city that continues to exist when you put the phone away, more neighbourhoods, broader culture and a practical base. Nice does not require you to become an extra in your own holiday.

Choose Cannes if...

You want compact, polished, beachy, hotel-heavy and a few more people who look as though they have just left a fitting room. Cannes knows exactly what it sells. Decades went into that packaging, gari, and not for dégun.

Sandy beaches are also a genuine advantage if you have a difficult relationship with Nice pebbles.

Our pick for a first trip

Nice, especially for three nights or more. More contrast, more life, better base. Cannes will survive our preference. Hu, it has known worse.

The useful question is not “which city is prettier?” but “what do you want to do once you get there?”. Less dramatic, much more helpful.
Independent editorial selection. Practical details change, sometimes between coffee and the bill.
