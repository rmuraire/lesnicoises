Nice or Cannes: where should you stay?

Nice or Cannes? Good news, neither city will collapse if you choose the other.

Choose Nice if...

You want a real city, more neighbourhoods to explore, broader culture and a very practical base for travelling east or west.

Choose Cannes if...

You want somewhere more compact, polished and focused on beaches, hotels and a particular idea of glamour. Cannes knows what it sells. It has had decades to work on the packaging.

Sandy beaches are also a genuine advantage if you have a difficult relationship with Nice pebbles.

Our pick for a first trip

Nice, especially for three nights or more. It has more contrasts and works better as a general Riviera base.

The useful question is not “which city is prettier?” but “what do you want to do once you get there?”. Less dramatic, much more helpful.
Independent editorial selection. Practical details change, sometimes between coffee and the bill.
