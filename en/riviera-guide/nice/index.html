RIVIERA GUIDE • NICE

Nice guide

Nice is beautiful enough to attract visitors and alive enough not to depend on them. That may be its best quality.

For a first trip, do not try to “do Nice”. The city works better as a sequence of neighbourhoods than a checklist of sights.

Old Nice, early

Go in the morning, before the streets completely fill. Cours Saleya, coloured facades, narrow lanes, then the sea. At noon in August you will understand why we specified morning.

The Port

A different mood without leaving the city. Terraces, boats, coloured buildings, then the coast towards Cap de Nice.

Castle Hill

For the view, obviously. There is no castle anymore. The name survived, which is already an impressive piece of property marketing.

Cimiez

For a different Nice: hills, architecture, gardens and a break from the coastal postcard.
