Choose the base before the hotel

The Riviera, by the trip you want.

Nice, Villefranche, Antibes, Cannes, Monaco and Menton share a railway line and very little personality. Choose what you want the holiday to feel like before opening the booking tabs.

First trip · start with NiceNo car · entirely realistic3–7 days · one base usually wins

The main decision

Same coast. Different trip.

The best base is not the most beautiful place in isolation. It is the place that makes the days you actually want easier.

Nice

Best first base. Transport, restaurants, culture and a city that remains alive after dinner.

Villefranche & Cap-Ferrat

Best for beauty and a slower pace. Fewer choices, considerably better excuses to do nothing.

Antibes

Best balance. Old town, sandy beach and useful rail links without Cannes-level performance.

Cannes

Best for polish and beach. Compact, sandy and unusually serious about the hotel being part of the holiday.

Monaco

Best when you have a reason to stay. Easy day trip; expensive base; very good at spectacle.

Menton

Best for the quiet eastern edge. Softer, slower and a natural counterpoint to Monaco.
