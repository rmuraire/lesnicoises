THE MAMETAS RIVIERA CHOOSER

Tell us the trip. We’ll cut the Riviera.

Four questions about geography and rhythm. No budget interrogation, no personality test and no list of 47 “perfect” options. Mametas chooses one base, the outings worth keeping and the part you can leave for another trip.

4 questionsOne base verdictEditorial rules, not a booking algorithm
The rule behind the tool

A question belongs here only if it can change where you sleep or where you go. Budget, hotel standing and restaurant spend come afterwards. Duration and mobility narrow the map; taste chooses among the survivors; pace changes how much hub efficiency matters.

01

How long do you actually have?

Three days and seven days are not the same Riviera with more pins.

02

How are you moving around?

This is a geography question, not a moral one. Sometimes the train wins. Sometimes it absolutely does not.

03

What do you want most from the Riviera?

Pick the thing you would actually notice if the trip failed to deliver it.

04

What pace feels like a holiday?

Pace does not vote for a town. It changes how much we value a base that can launch you elsewhere efficiently.
