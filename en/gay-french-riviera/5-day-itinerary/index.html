5 DAYS · NICE BASE · LGBTQ+ TRAVEL

Five days on the French Riviera, gay and uncomplicated

One hotel in Nice. Four directions. No attempt to turn every hour into specifically gay tourism. We add the LGBTQ+ context where it helps, then get back to the sea.

BaseNice for all five nights.
TransportTrain, tram and walking.
LGBTQ+ logicUse Nice for the community layer; use the Riviera for the Riviera.

Day 1 — Nice first

Cours Saleya, Old Nice, Castle Hill, lunch, the Port and a swim. In the evening, check the current LGBTQIA+ calendar if you want a dedicated venue or event. Open the Gay Nice guide →

Day 2 — Nice, slower

Choose one cultural stop, then give the afternoon to the sea. Castel Plage is currently Nice Rainbow-labelled if that signal matters.

Day 3 — Villefranche + Cap-Ferrat

Go east for the bay, old streets and a slower rhythm. Continue towards Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat if energy and weather agree. This is exactly the kind of day where the LGBTQ+ part becomes irrelevant — which is rather the point.

Day 4 — Antibes, then Cannes

Train west. Give Antibes enough time for the old town and sea. Continue to Cannes later if the Croisette appeals. Return to Nice at night if you want its LGBTQ+ scene.

Day 5 — Monaco + Menton, or refuse to rush

They pair logically by train. But another beach morning may be a better use of your remaining life. The itinerary will survive your disobedience.

THE POINT

The useful gay itinerary is not a separate Riviera. It is a very good Riviera itinerary with the right base and reliable LGBTQ+ information.
