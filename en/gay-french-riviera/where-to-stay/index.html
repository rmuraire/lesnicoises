FRENCH RIVIERA · LGBTQ+ · WHERE TO STAY

Gay French Riviera: where should you actually stay?

The useful choice is not “gay hotel or normal hotel?”. It is whether you want a city base, an explicitly gay stay, or the western Riviera on your doorstep. Those are three different holidays.

Best first base: NiceNo car, strongest LGBTQ+ infrastructure, easiest trains east and west.
Best explicit gay stayLa Connexion, inland from Cannes, if a men-only retreat matters more than city logistics.
Cannes wins on geographyChoose it when the west — Antibes, Lérins, Grasse, Esterel — is the real trip.
THE MAMETAS TAKE

For most first-time gay travellers: stay in Nice. If the accommodation itself is the point, a dedicated gay guesthouse can beat the city. If the western Riviera is the point, Cannes can beat both.

1. Nice: the default that is actually useful

Nice gives you the official Nice Rainbow network, the coast’s most visible LGBTQ+ nightlife and the cleanest no-car logistics. That matters because you can choose when the gay layer matters instead of organising every day around it.

For a conventional hotel, use the four Nice Rainbow hotels already selected in our Gay Nice guide. They are mainstream hotels with an explicit inclusion standard, not “gay hotels”. That distinction matters.

2. If you want a genuine gay guesthouse in Nice

Blue Angels Bed & Breakfast — Nice

8 rue Assalit · central Nice

The clearest answer if you specifically want a gay stay rather than a gay-friendly hotel. The tourist office describes it as a 100% gay bed & breakfast with two rooms and a maximum capacity of four.

Nice Rainbow · men only · small-scale · direct booking. At our September 2026 check we did not find a current Booking.com listing, so Mametas does not attach an affiliate button.

Les Jardins de Baquis — Nice

3 avenue Baquis · central Nice

A single quiet gay-friendly guest room with a private bathroom and balcony, within walking distance of the beach and centre. Much closer to staying with a local than checking into a hotel.

Nice Rainbow · one room · direct booking. Again, no Booking affiliate button unless a current Booking listing appears.

3. The Booking-listed gay stay: La Connexion

La Connexion, Gay Men Only — Peymeinade

Near Grasse / about 22 km from Cannes · car strongly recommended

This is the opposite of a Nice city base: adults-only, gay-men-only, pool, garden, restaurant and a retreat format in the hills. Booking currently lists it, and the property is also presented by specialist gay-travel sources as a gay-owned, men-only resort.

Choose it when the stay itself is part of the holiday. Do not choose it for a five-day no-car Riviera sprint. Booking lists Nice airport at roughly 40 km away and free on-site parking.

4. Nice or Cannes for a gay couple?

Nice if you want bars, community infrastructure, no-car day trips and one base for the whole coast. Cannes if beaches, Lérins, Antibes and the western Riviera dominate the plan and nightlife matters less.

The mistake is choosing Cannes because it looks more glamorous, then spending every evening on a train to Nice. The opposite mistake is sleeping in Nice while every saved place on your map is west of Antibes.

5. With a car vs without one

No carNice. The answer is almost boringly clear.
Car + community sceneStill Nice, unless parking becomes the dominant problem.
Car + retreatLa Connexion becomes a credible alternative.
