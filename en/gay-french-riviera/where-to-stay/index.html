FRENCH RIVIERA · LGBTQ+ · WHERE TO STAY

Gay French Riviera: where should you actually stay?

The useful choice is not “gay hotel or normal hotel?”. It is whether you want a city base, an explicitly gay stay, or the western Riviera on your doorstep. Those are three different holidays.

Best first base: NiceNo car, strongest LGBTQ+ infrastructure, easiest trains east and west.
Best explicit gay stayLa Connexion, inland from Cannes, if a men-only retreat matters more than city logistics.
Cannes wins on geographyChoose it when the west — Antibes, Lérins, Grasse, Esterel — is the real trip.
THE MAMETAS TAKE

For most first-time gay travellers: stay in Nice. If the accommodation itself is the point, a dedicated gay guesthouse can beat the city. If the western Riviera is the point, Cannes can beat both.

Choose the type of stay first

Nice Rainbow Côte d’Azur is a welcome label, not a promise that the hotel clientele will be predominantly gay. The current official directory lists 34 accommodation options. Mametas uses the label as one useful signal, then sorts stays by the trip they actually create.

Gay-only or community stay

Mametas take: choose this profile only if the accommodation itself is part of the LGBTQ+ experience. Otherwise, a central Nice hotel is usually easier.

Nice or Cannes?

Nice if you want bars, community infrastructure, no-car day trips and one base for the whole coast. Cannes if beaches, Lérins, Antibes and the western Riviera dominate the plan and nightlife matters less.

With a car vs without one

No carNice. The answer is almost boringly clear.
Car + community sceneStill Nice, unless parking becomes the dominant problem.
Car + retreatLa Connexion becomes a credible alternative.

FAQ

What is the Nice Rainbow label?

An official welcome programme used by Nice Côte d’Azur. It is a useful signal of explicit LGBTQ+ welcome; it does not mean the hotel is gay-only or that most guests will be LGBTQ+.

Are there gay-only hotels near Nice?

The clearest dedicated option in this guide is La Connexion, inland from Cannes. In Nice itself, Blue Angels is a very small men-only B&B rather than a conventional hotel.

Which area of Nice is best for gay nightlife?

Old Nice, Garibaldi and the Port side keep the useful evening addresses closest. If nightlife matters, prioritise walkability over a seafront postcode.
