Gay French Riviera: the useful version

Sea, good hotels, old towns, long lunches — plus the LGBTQ+ context that actually changes a travel decision. No parallel Riviera required.

Nice is the obvious starting point if LGBTQ+ context matters to you. The tourism board runs Nice Rainbow, is a member of IGLTA and maintains dedicated gay-friendly accommodation and event information. Useful, yes. A reason to turn every meal and museum into “gay tourism”, no.

THE MAMETAS TAKE

Choose Nice as the base, then do the Riviera. Villefranche, Cap-Ferrat, Antibes, Cannes, Monaco and Menton remain the same places; you simply sleep in the city with the strongest transport and the most visible LGBTQ+ ecosystem.

Where to stay

Masséna / Carré d’Or is the easy all-round answer. Port / Garibaldi gives you more neighbourhood texture and evening life. Nice-Ville / Jean Médecin is the rational choice for train-heavy days. Use Nice Rainbow as an extra confidence signal, not your only hotel filter.

Beaches

Castel Plage is currently listed with the Nice Rainbow label. Otherwise choose the beach for the beach. Pebbles remain stubbornly non-discriminatory. For prettier water and a slower day, go east towards Villefranche-sur-Mer and Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat.

Evenings

Nice has LGBTQ+ venues, associations and events, but those details change faster than a static “top 10 gay bars” article. Check the official live calendar and the Centre LGBTQIA+ Côte d’Azur, then decide whether you want a community night, a mainstream restaurant or both.
