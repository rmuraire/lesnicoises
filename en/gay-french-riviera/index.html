FRENCH RIVIERA · LGBTQ+ TRAVEL

Gay French Riviera: the useful version

The useful question is not “where is the gay Riviera?” It is “where should I base myself, and how do I use the coast without turning the whole trip into LGBTQ+ logistics?”

Best single base: NiceStrong transport, a visible LGBTQ+ ecosystem and the easiest access to both sides of the coast.
West: Antibes + CannesEasy to fold into the trip from Nice; worth a different base only if the western Riviera is your main event.
East: Monaco + MentonSimple train days from Nice. No need to invent a separate LGBTQ+ itinerary for each stop.

Nice is where the LGBTQ+ infrastructure on this stretch of the Riviera is most explicit. The tourism board runs Nice Rainbow, belongs to IGLTA and reported about 80 labelled places in its 2026 material, including 34 accommodation options. That is why Mametas uses Nice as the anchor for this guide. The rest of the coast is then a geography problem, not an identity problem.

THE MAMETAS TAKE

For a first trip, sleep in Nice and travel out. It gives you the community layer when you want it, while keeping Villefranche, Cap-Ferrat, Antibes, Cannes, Monaco and Menton within easy reach.

When the LGBTQ+ calendar matters

Nice is the place to check for the annual layer: Pink Parade, IN&OUT, Dolly Party, Carnaval season and the summer programme. Smaller parties and community events change week by week, so use the live calendar rather than a frozen list.

FAQ

Is the French Riviera gay friendly?

Nice has the clearest formal LGBTQ+ infrastructure on this stretch of the coast. Elsewhere, the practical question is usually where you want to spend the day rather than whether you need a separate gay itinerary.

Nice or Cannes for gay travellers?

Nice for the strongest community layer and easiest no-car base. Cannes when the western Riviera is the main purpose of the trip.

Is Monaco gay friendly?

For most visitors, Monaco works as a day trip rather than an LGBTQ+ destination. Use Nice for the scene and Monaco for Monaco.

When is Nice Pride?

Dates can move. Check the current official LGBTQIA+ calendar before booking around a specific event.

Do I need a car?

Not for a Nice-based first trip. Trains, trams and walking cover the core coast efficiently; a car becomes more useful for inland stays such as La Connexion.
