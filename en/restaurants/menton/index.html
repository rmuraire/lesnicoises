RESTAURANTS • MENTON

Where to eat in Menton: 2 addresses, two ways to see Mauro Colagreco

In Menton, you can lunch facing Italy and dine with one of the world’s most famous chefs. You can also order a random pizza. Freedom is a strange thing, pichoun.

MAMETAS SAYS

Two budgets, two experiences, the same chef behind the ecosystem. Do not compare the bills. It would tire everyone.

30 avenue Aristide Briand

Mirazur

For the big night. The real one, not merely the evening when you wore a white shirt.

Mauro Colagreco’s three-star restaurant builds its cooking around its gardens, with five hectares described as biodynamic and permaculture. It is a gastronomic destination in itself, not somewhere you choose because it happens to be on the way.

Open Tuesday to Sunday during summer 2026 according to the official site. Reservations are released in stages. Plan ahead. For once, really.

Official source
80 bis boulevard de Garavan

Casa Fuego

For wood fire, Argentina and a much less ceremonial evening.

Mauro Colagreco’s family-style restaurant revolves around the Argentinian grill and asado, overlooking the Mediterranean and Menton old town. More relaxed, without becoming careless.

80 bis boulevard de Garavan. Closed Monday and Tuesday according to opening times displayed in August 2026.

Official source

And no, being two kilometres from Italy does not require eating Italian food three times a day. The border is open. Your imagination may be too.
