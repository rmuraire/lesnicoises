Where to eat in Grasse

Five useful choices: vegan in town, a small bistronomic table, unfussy seafood, a panoramic terrace or the grand Provençal house.

MAMETAS SAYS

Grasse does not need a solemn gastronomic list. It needs options that fit the day you are actually having.

Price: € simple · €€ moderate · €€€ expensive / occasion · €€€€ big night. Relative positioning.

8 avenue Maximin Isnard · € · vegan · town centre

Le Poulpi Place

Choose it for: a simple plant-based pause that is genuinely different from the rest of the list.

A 100% vegan bar, café and restaurant with home-made food, open through a broad part of 2026.

Official source
2 avenue Chiris · €€ · bistronomic · small dining room

La Fleur de Lys

Choose it for: a compact town restaurant between traditional French and bistronomic cooking.

About thirty seats and a location that works well if your day stays centred on Grasse.

Official source
321 route de Cannes · €€ · fish / seafood · convivial bistro

Le Cabanon de l’Écailler

Choose it for: fresh fish and seafood without palace vocabulary.

Since 2009, the house has worked in a friendly cabanon spirit with catch of the day, oysters and shellfish. The weekday lunch special was listed at €17 when checked.

Official source
381 route de Plascassier · €€€ · seasonal cooking · panoramic terrace

Lougolin

Choose it for: inventive bistro cooking and the terrace facing the Estérel.

Xavier Malandran works with seasonal cooking and very little ceremony. Weekday lunch remains the more approachable way into this register.

Official source
48 avenue Henri Dunant · €€€€ · gastronomic · bastide

La Bastide Saint-Antoine

Choose it for: the occasion meal in a grand Provençal house.

Jacques Chibois's bastide above Grasse is a destination in its own right, with gastronomic cooking and a deep cellar.

The catch: this is the meal you organise the day around, not the one you add after a perfume visit.

Official source

20-second decision: vegan and simple - Poulpi. Small town table - Fleur de Lys. Friendly seafood - Cabanon de l’Écailler. Terrace and seasonal cooking - Lougolin. Big meal - Bastide Saint-Antoine.
