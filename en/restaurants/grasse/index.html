RESTAURANTS • GRASSE

Where to eat in Grasse

People come to Grasse for perfume. It would be a shame to eat as if your sense of smell stopped at the perfume-shop door.

MAMETAS SAYS

Bastide Saint-Antoine for an occasion. Lougolin for the terrace and a more grounded price point. Fleur de Lys when you want to stay in town.

48 avenue Henri Dunant

La Bastide Saint-Antoine

Pour la grande maison provençale de Jacques Chibois.

A Relais & Châteaux bastide overlooking the hinterland and Bay of Cannes, with Jacques Chibois’s long-established gastronomic cooking and a famously deep cellar.

Official source
381 route de Plascassier

Lougolin

Pour la terrasse panoramique et une cuisine de saison sans cérémonie inutile.

Chef Xavier Malandran cooks a seasonal, inventive bistro menu with a broad view towards the Estérel and the Bay of Théoule. The weekday lunch menu is one of the more approachable ways into the area’s serious food scene.

Official source
2 avenue Chiris

La Fleur de Lys

Pour une petite table bistronomique dans Grasse.

A compact 30-seat restaurant with traditional French and bistronomic cooking, listed by the regional tourism office and open through most of 2026.

Official source
