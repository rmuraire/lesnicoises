Where to eat in Nice: five places worth a look

A first selection of five places. Not 'the five best restaurants in Nice'. Definitive rankings are very convenient, especially for the people writing them.

Selection checked in August 2026 using restaurant websites, the Nice Côte d’Azur Tourist Office and professional guides. We do not pretend to have eaten somewhere when we have not. Opening hours change. Hunger is more reliable.
RUE RAOUL BOSIO

La Merenda

A small Old Nice address strongly associated with traditional Niçoise cooking. Come for the local dishes, not the furniture.

RUE BAVASTRO

Chez Pipo

Socca is the obvious reason. The address has been part of Nice for generations. Socca did not need reinventing. It was doing perfectly well.

RUE DROITE

Chez Acchiardo

A family-run Old Nice institution rooted in local and Mediterranean classics. The room does not spend much time convincing Instagram that it has a personality.

RUE DE LA PRÉFECTURE

Le Bistrot d’Antoine

An established French bistrot in Old Nice, known for straightforward market cooking and a classic atmosphere.

RUE JACQUES MÉDECIN

Peixes

For something more contemporary: fish, Mediterranean flavours and South American influence. Useful once you have completed your socca obligations.

Check before you go: La Merenda · Chez Pipo · Acchiardo · Bistrot d’Antoine · Peixes.
Independent editorial selection. Practical details change, sometimes between coffee and the bill.
