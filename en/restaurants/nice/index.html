RESTAURANTS • NICE

Where to eat in Nice : 6 places worth a look

You can eat extremely well in Nice. You can also get fleeced beside the sea over a €26 burrata. Mèfi, pichoun.

We are not calling these the six best restaurants in Nice. Anyone ranking daube, fish and pasta with scientific confidence spends too much time online. And if you pick dinner because the wicker chairs look good on Instagram, do not come crying about the bill. Here are six places with six good reasons to go.

MÈFI, PICHOUN

If the waiter is fishing for you from the pavement, the menu has the thickness of a phone book and every dish has its own photograph, listen to the warning. Dégun needs 112 dishes to cook well. And the sea view still cannot cook a fish.

4 RUE RAOUL BOSIO • OLD NICE

La Mérenda

For Niçoise cooking with very little theatre.

A tiny 24-seat bistrot run by Dominique Le Stanc, formerly chef at the Negresco. Local recipes, very small room, no need for decorative fog.

The tourist office lists Tuesday to Friday opening in 2026.

13 RUE BAVASTRO • PORT

Chez Pipo

For socca. No further explanation required.

Founded in 1923 and still built around wood-fired socca and Niçoise specialities. Simple, local and far more useful than a “deconstructed” version on an enormous plate with three drops of sauce. Hu. Leave the socca alone.

The official site lists Tuesday to Sunday opening.

38 RUE DROITE • OLD NICE

Chez Acchiardo

For a family-run classic in the middle of Old Nice.

Mediterranean and Niçoise cooking in a traditional setting. Sometimes it is a relief to order something whose name fits on one line.

27 RUE DE LA PRÉFECTURE • OLD NICE

Le Bistrot d’Antoine

For proper bistrot cooking with less folklore.

Rated a “Table Gourmande” by Gault&Millau in 2026. A changing chalkboard, southern flavours and the busy atmosphere of rue de la Préfecture.

4 RUE JACQUES MÉDECIN • OPÉRA

Peixes

For contemporary fish once you have completed your socca obligations.

Fish and seafood with Mediterranean and South American influence, including ceviche, carpaccio, tartare and tataki. Gault&Millau gives it 12.5/20 in 2026.

The official site lists the Opéra location as open Monday to Saturday, noon to 10 pm non-stop.

6 AVENUE ALFRED BORRIGLIONE • LIBÉRATION

Socca Tram

For socca away from the permanent theatre of Old Nice.

A straightforward option in the Libération neighbourhood. You do not need to cross the entire city every time chickpea flour becomes a priority.

What to order first

Socca to share, petits farcis, pissaladière, then fish or daube depending on where you are. And yes, chard pie can be sweet. Nice made that decision before you arrived and has survived worse opinions than yours. You can fuss over the recipe like a ficanas, gari. It is not changing for you.
