RESTAURANTS • SAINT-PAUL-DE-VENCE

Where to eat in Saint-Paul-de-Vence

La Colombe d’Or for the legend. Three simpler choices for days when lunch does not need to become a historical monument.

MAMETAS SAYS

The village sells scenery very efficiently. So we separate the destination restaurant from the places where you can simply eat well without treating every bill as a souvenir.

Institution · grand dinner

La Colombe d’Or

For modern art, history and Provençal cooking in a room that knows exactly why people came.

Picasso, Miró, Matisse, Braque, Léger and Calder are part of the house’s story. Book it because the restaurant itself is a reason to come.

Official source
Light lunch · gentler budget

Café Timothé

The useful counterpoint: Mediterranean food, mostly organic and local produce, plus vegetarian and gluten-free choices.

At the time of our check, the tourism office listed the daily dish at €16 and dessert at €6. Saint-Paul can, apparently, host lunch without requiring a commemorative occasion.

Official source
Bistro · strong value

Le Tilleul

For a proper, fresh bistro meal without jumping straight into fine-dining territory.

The official listing showed suggestions from €16 and menus at €32 and €39 when checked. It is the most versatile pick here.

Official source
View · Provençal cooking · parking

La Terrasse sur Saint-Paul

For the panorama and the practicalities, with traditional Provençal cooking in the middle register.

The official listing showed a daily dish from €18 and à-la-carte prices from €20 when checked. On-site parking is less romantic than a panoramic terrace, which is precisely why it is useful.

Official source

Prices move. The point of this list is not to freeze a bill in time, but to stop four recommendations from doing the exact same job.
