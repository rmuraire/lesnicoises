Where to eat in Saint-Paul-de-Vence

Four places are enough here: one institution, one versatile bistro, one light lunch and one practical terrace with a view.

MAMETAS SAYS

The village sells scenery very efficiently. We separate the destination meal from the table that simply does its job well.

La Colombe d’Or

Choose it for: history, art and a meal that becomes a visit in its own right.

Picasso, Miró, Matisse, Braque, Léger and Calder are part of the house's story. Book it because it is a reason to come, not because it happens to be on the way.

Official source
Village · € · Mediterranean · light lunch

Café Timothé

Choose it for: a simple pause, mostly organic and local produce, plus vegetarian and gluten-free options.

When checked, the tourism office listed the daily dish at €16 and dessert at €6.

Official source
Village · €€ · bistro · versatile

Le Tilleul

Choose it for: a proper fresh bistro meal without jumping into fine-dining territory.

The official listing showed suggestions from €16 and menus at €32 and €39 when checked. It is the easiest recommendation here for most people.

Official source
Village entrance · €€ · Provençal · view / parking

La Terrasse sur Saint-Paul

Choose it for: the panorama and the practicalities, especially if you arrive by car.

The official listing showed a daily dish from €18 and à-la-carte prices from €20 when checked. On-site parking is less romantic than the view, which is exactly why it is useful.

Official source

20-second decision: light lunch - Café Timothé. All-round bistro - Le Tilleul. View and parking - La Terrasse sur Saint-Paul. Destination meal - La Colombe d’Or.
