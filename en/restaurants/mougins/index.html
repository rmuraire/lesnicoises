RESTAURANTS • MOUGINS

Where to eat in Mougins

Mougins has long carried the label of a gastronomic village. There is no need to turn lunch into a heritage exam. Two tables are enough to start.

MAMETAS SAYS

L’Amandier for history and Provence. Le Candela for a more contemporary, evening-driven Riviera mood.

48 avenue Jean-Charles Mallet

L’Amandier de Mougins

Pour la Provence et la vue depuis le vieux village.

Founded in 1970 and historically associated with Roger Vergé, L’Amandier now presents Provençal-Mediterranean cooking with two panoramic terraces over the Plan de Grasse.

Official source
172 boulevard Clément Rebuffel, Le Mas Candille

Le Candela

Pour un dîner méditerranéen plus contemporain.

At the reborn Mas Candille, Le Candela leans into olive oil, garden herbs, tomatoes and handmade pasta, with a polished but deliberately relaxed evening atmosphere.
