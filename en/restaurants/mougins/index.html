Where to eat in Mougins

Mougins has long cultivated its gastronomic-village reputation. Four places are enough to separate the family bistro, panoramic Provence, product-led cooking and the more contemporary dinner.

MAMETAS SAYS

Do not choose "a restaurant in Mougins". Choose the kind of evening: old stone, seasonal cooking, terrace or a more polished night out.

Le Rendez-vous de Mougins

Choose it for: a warm meal in the heart of the village, with a menu that moves with the market.

Taken over in 2026 by a restaurant family, it focuses on fresh produce, dishes made to order and deliberately generous, approachable cooking.

48 avenue Jean-Charles Mallet · €€€ · Provençal / Mediterranean · panorama

L’Amandier de Mougins

Choose it for: the village's gastronomic history and panoramic terraces.

Founded in 1970 and historically associated with Roger Vergé, L’Amandier remains the classic Provençal address in old Mougins.

Official sourceMap ↗
41 place du Commandant Lamy · €€€ · product-led · occasion

La Place de Mougins

Choose it for: cooking built around a seasonal product in the centre of the village.

Denis Fétisson focuses each month on one ingredient from starter to dessert. A clear concept if you want a chef-led experience rather than a passing dinner.

Le Mas Candille · €€€ · Mediterranean · contemporary evening

Le Candela

Choose it for: a more contemporary Mediterranean dinner under the pines, away from the museum-piece mood of the village.

Le Candela leans into southern flavours, olive oil, herbs, tomatoes and handmade pasta in a more evening-driven setting.
