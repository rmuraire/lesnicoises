Where to eat in Antibes

Old Antibes, Juan-les-Pins, Cap d’Antibes: “Antibes” hides several very different evenings. Ten addresses are enough to separate them without writing an encyclopaedia.

MAMETAS SAYS

Do not choose the same restaurant after a Juan-les-Pins beach day, a day in Old Antibes and a big night on the Cap. Same town, different brief.
