Where to eat in Théoule-sur-Mer

Four ways to use the Estérel scenery: easy lunch, polished beach table, serious seafood or destination dinner. The view is free. The bill is less cooperative.

MAMETAS SAYS

Théoule is small. The useful list should be short too, but the choices need to do genuinely different jobs.

La Terrasse de Théoule

Choose it for: an easy meal without turning every sea-view lunch into a ceremony.

Seafood, mussels and fries, pizzas, burgers and salads, with all-day service advertised year-round. It is the practical choice in the list, which is exactly why the list needs it.

Promenade de la Darse · €€€ · beach · Italian / Mediterranean

La Plage Blanche

Choose it for: lunch or dinner by the water with a proper level of service, without jumping straight to a tasting menu.

The Château de Théoule beach table works with antipasti, fresh pasta and grilled catch of the day in a polished but relaxed setting.

Official source
16 avenue du Trayas · €€€ · seafood · panoramic

La Maréa

Choose it for: fish as the main subject, not an excuse for the view.

A seafood-focused house with a panoramic dining room and terrace. A strong choice when you want Théoule to remain a coastal day but still want a real meal in the middle of it.

Official source
55 avenue de Lérins, Château de Théoule · €€€€ · gastronomic · destination

Mareluna

Choose it for: a gastronomic experience that justifies building the evening around it.

Francesco Fezza combines Italian roots, Japanese technique and Mediterranean produce across several tasting menus.

The catch: this is not dinner after the beach. It is the dinner that replaces the rest.

Official source

20-second decision: easy and practical - La Terrasse. Polished beach meal - La Plage Blanche. Serious seafood - La Maréa. Big dinner - Mareluna.
