RESTAURANTS • THÉOULE-SUR-MER

Where to eat in Théoule-sur-Mer

Théoule has an unfair advantage: the red Estérel rocks fall into the Mediterranean behind your plate. We still checked that the plate has a reason to be there.

MAMETAS SAYS

Mareluna for a proper gastronomic experience. La Plage Blanche when you want the horizon to do half the work.

55 avenue de Lérins, Château de Théoule

Mareluna

Pour l’étoile Michelin et une cuisine entre Italie, Japon et Méditerranée.

Chef Francesco Fezza combines Italian roots, Japanese technique and local Mediterranean products in tasting menus that change with the seasons. Michelin-starred since 2025.

Official source
Promenade de la Darse

La Plage Blanche

Pour déjeuner ou dîner au bord de l’eau sans passer directement au menu dégustation.

The Château de Théoule’s beach restaurant serves a seasonal Italian-leaning menu: antipasti, fresh pasta and grilled catch of the day, with the sea doing what the sea does best in the background.
