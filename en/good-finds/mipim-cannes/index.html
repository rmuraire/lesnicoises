MIPIM Cannes 2027: where to stay when the whole city has the same idea

MIPIM is a real-estate conference. For four days, Cannes applies the concept of scarcity to its own hotel rooms.

THE MAMETAS TAKE

For MIPIM, pay for geography before stars. Palais/Croisette if you want to walk everywhere. Station area for the best compromise. Antibes is intelligent overflow. Nice is viable, less charming after a late dinner.

MIPIM 2027 slightly changes the map

The Palais remains the core, but MIPIM also announces a Tech & AI Hub at the Gray d’Albion and Luxury Living & Branded Residences on the JW Marriott beach.

Nice: viable, with one rule

SNCF shows an average journey of about 35 minutes between Nice and Cannes. Fine if your day ends early; less fine when evenings are part of the event.

MIPIM advertises a free shuttle from hotels around Cannes to the Palais. Check the 2027 hotel list and timetable before relying on an older arrangement.
