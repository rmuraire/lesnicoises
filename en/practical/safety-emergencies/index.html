PRACTICAL RIVIERA · SAFETY

If something goes wrong on the Riviera

The numbers to save, who to call and the next useful step - without turning a holiday into a risk manual.

EUROPEAN EMERGENCY112

General emergency number across the EU. Free, 24/7.

POLICE17

Police or gendarmerie when rapid intervention is needed.

MEDICAL15

SAMU for urgent medical assistance.

FIRE / RESCUE18

Fire brigade and rescue services on land.

CAN'T SPEAK ALOUD114

Emergency text/app service, also for deaf or hard-of-hearing people.

AT SEA / FROM THE COAST196

CROSS sea rescue. At sea, VHF channel 16 remains the preferred contact.

THE SIMPLE RULE

Immediate danger first, paperwork second. Call 112/17/15/18 as appropriate. If it is not an emergency, use the nearest police station or gendarmerie for reporting and follow-up.

If a phone, wallet or documents are stolen

  1. Block bank cards and the SIM/eSIM quickly.
  2. Secure the phone account and change critical passwords from another device.
  3. Report theft to police or gendarmerie; for a non-urgent case, use the nearest station rather than 17.
  4. Keep a copy of your passport/ID and hotel address somewhere other than the phone itself.

Victim support

116 006 is the national victim-support line in mainland France, free and available every day from 09:00 to 20:00. 3919 is a free, anonymous 24/7 listening and orientation service for women facing sexist or sexual violence; it is not an emergency line. In immediate danger, use 17, 18 or 112.

Digital backup

French cyber-security guidance recommends avoiding sensitive transactions on public or unknown Wi-Fi where possible. Save the hotel address, emergency bank contacts and one trusted contact offline or somewhere separate from the phone.
