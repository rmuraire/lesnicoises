Where should you stay in Nice?

01 · Practical

You are here to use Nice as a base.

Prioritise tram access, a reasonable route to Nice-Ville and an easy walk home. For three to seven days without a car, logistics outperform a theoretical sea view.

The catch: no pool and no sea-view theatre.

02 · Lively

You want dinner and atmosphere outside the door.

The catch: slopes, luggage and a formidable price.

You want Nice, but not all of it in your bedroom.
