Internal utility

Mametas Reporting

Mametas Reporting is a private analytics utility for the Mametas website. It is not an account, service or login offered to readers.

What it does

With the website operator's Google authorisation, a Google Apps Script reads traffic and acquisition metrics from Google Analytics 4 and search performance metrics from Google Search Console. It writes selected report tables to a Google Sheets spreadsheet controlled by the website operator.

The reports include aggregated website sessions, landing pages, search queries, clicks, impressions and daily trends. A scheduled daily refresh replaces the current Google reporting tables. No third-party analytics aggregator is required.

Access and security

Google permissions are granted through Google's own consent process. Analytics and Search Console are accessed in read-only mode. The Google Sheets permission permits editing spreadsheets, but the script is configured to write to one identified reporting spreadsheet rather than other files.

The script does not ask for Google passwords and does not transmit OAuth access tokens to the Mametas website. Its reporting data stays within the Google services used by the authorised account.

Privacy

For the categories of Google data accessed, their use, storage, retention and how to revoke access, see the Mametas Reporting privacy notice.

This internal utility is distinct from the visitor-facing Mametas website. Its website privacy & cookies policy separately explains cookies and analytics consent for visitors.
