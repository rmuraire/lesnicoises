Where to stay in Nice

A beautiful hotel can still be the wrong hotel.

Nice is our default first-trip base. Our cards start with the use case and end with the catch. Rates change; geography and compromises tend to be more reliable.

Choose your Nice hotel
Best for · atmosphere

Hôtel du Couvent

A remarkable hideaway in Old Nice when the hotel is part of the trip, not merely where you leave the suitcase.

The catch: charm, slopes and heavy luggage are not always close friends.
Best for · smart logistics

Hôtel Apollinaire

Central, useful and considerably easier than organising every day around a glamorous sea view.

The catch: this is the practical choice, not the toes-in-the-water fantasy.
Best for · romantic Nice

Hôtel La Pérouse

Above the sea at the edge of Old Nice, with the view doing a respectable amount of work.

The catch: the main railway station is not your neighbour.
